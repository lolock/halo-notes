Anthropic 正在扩展 Claude 认证计划，新增三项基于角色的认证，覆盖客户部署 AI 所需的完整团队角色。Anthropic 的目标是成为 AI enablement 领域的领导者。为实现这一目标，我们于 3 月推出了 Claude Partner Network，并配套投入 1 亿美元；6 月又推出了 Services Track，帮助企业买家找到合适的机构，让 Claude 真正应用于其业务之中。

帮助企业找到合适机构的一个重要环节，是确保这些机构的人员经过适当认证。我们希望 Claude 认证成为客户、合作伙伴和整个行业都认可的质量基准，代表经过验证的专业能力，而不仅仅是一枚徽章。为此，Claude 认证通过有监考且经过身份验证的考试授予，考查特定角色所要求的技能。

这种做法正在迅速获得认可。自 3 月推出以来，已有超过 36,000 名顾问在 1,300 多家机构中获得认证。今年，超过 400,000 人通过 Anthropic Partner Academy（我们面向合作伙伴的免费培训平台）完成了 Claude 培训。

以下是扩展后的认证计划需要了解的内容。

<!-- lang:en -->

We're expanding the Claude Certification Program with three new role-based certifications that span the full team a customer needs to deploy AI. Anthropic's goal is to be the leader of AI enablement. In support of this goal, we launched the Claude Partner Network in March alongside a $100 million investment and a Services Track in June to help enterprise buyers find the right firms to put Claude to work inside their business.

To achieve this, Claude certifications are granted through supervised, identity-verified exams that test the specific skills a role calls for.

This approach is quickly gaining traction. Since launching in March, more than 36,000 consultants have received certification across more than 1,300 organizations. More than 400,000 people have completed Claude training this year through Anthropic Partner Academy, our free training platform for partners.

Here's what you need to know about our expanded certification program.

## 考试如何运作 / How the exams work

<!-- bilingual:section -->

<!-- lang:zh -->

我们与 Pearson 合作，借助其全球评估与认证基础设施来实施这一计划。每场考试都在监考下进行，也就是在监督下完成，并通过 Pearson Professional Assessments 提供。考试受到安全管理，考生必须在开始考试前验证身份。通过考试的人将通过 Pearson 旗下的 Credly 获得数字徽章。

> “AI 的发展速度超过了大多数人建立足够技能、充满信心地使用它的速度，而这一差距正是经过验证的评估旨在弥合的。Anthropic 建立了 AI 实验室中首个有监考的认证体系，使用与 Pearson 在专业执照认证中所采用的相同可信基础设施，来评估技能、颁发凭证并验证成果。这再次说明，Anthropic 为什么是全球企业值得信赖的 AI 合作伙伴。”
>
> —— Vishaal Gupta，Pearson 企业学习与技能部门总裁

监考和身份验证之所以重要，是因为这些认证会被用于决策。获得认证的从业者人数决定着公司在 Claude Partner Network 中的地位；企业审查哪些人员将参与其系统的工作时，这一数字也具有参考意义。只有当评估严格、能够验证个人的实际应用技能时，认证才能为这些决策提供可靠依据。

我们的认证正在负责为客户实施 Claude 的咨询和工程公司内部获得越来越多的认可。认证也决定着公司如何在该网络中晋级。最高级别 Global Premier 要求拥有 1,000 名认证从业者、覆盖三个地区的 100 个客户，以及 15 份公开的客户背书。下一次在全网评估这些标准的等级审核将于 10 月 1 日进行。

<!-- lang:en -->

We work with Pearson, drawing on its global assessment and credentialing infrastructure, to deliver our program. Every exam is proctored, meaning it is taken under supervision, and delivered through Pearson Professional Assessments. Exams are securely administered, and test takers must validate their identity before beginning an exam. Those who pass the exam receive a digital badge through Credly by Pearson.

> "AI is moving faster than most people's ability to build the skills to use it confidently, and that gap is exactly what verified assessment is built to close. Anthropic has built the first proctored certification among AI labs that uses the same trusted infrastructure for skills, credentials, and verified outcomes that Pearson uses across professional licensure. This is another example that shows why Anthropic is a trusted AI partner for businesses globally."
>
> — Vishaal Gupta, President, Enterprise Learning and Skills, Pearson

Proctoring and identity verification matter because these credentials are used to make decisions. Certified practitioner counts determine a firm's standing in the Claude Partner Network and are relevant when companies vet who will work on their systems. A credential can only support those decisions if the assessment is rigorous and validates a person's applied skills.

Our certification is gaining momentum inside the consulting and engineering firms implementing Claude for their clients. Certifications also determine how firms advance in the network. The top tier, Global Premier, requires 1,000 certified practitioners, 100 customers across three regions, and 15 public customer endorsements. The next tier review where this criteria will be evaluated across the network is on October 1.
