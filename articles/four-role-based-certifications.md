## 考试如何运作 / How the exams work

> EN: We work with Pearson, drawing on its global assessment and credentialing infrastructure, to deliver our program. Every exam is proctored, meaning it is taken under supervision, and delivered through Pearson Professional Assessments. Exams are securely administered, and test takers must validate their identity before beginning an exam. Those who pass the exam receive a digital badge through Credly by Pearson.

ZH：我们与 Pearson 合作，借助其全球评估和认证基础设施来交付我们的计划。每场考试都有监考人监督，通过 Pearson Professional Assessments 进行。考试过程安全受控，考生在开始考试前必须验证身份。通过考试的人将获得由 Pearson 旗下 Credly 颁发的数字徽章。

> EN: "AI is moving faster than most people's ability to build the skills to use it confidently, and that gap is exactly what verified assessment is built to close. Anthropic has built the first proctored certification among AI labs that uses the same trusted infrastructure for skills, credentials, and verified outcomes that Pearson uses across professional licensure. This is another example that shows why Anthropic is a trusted AI partner for businesses globally."

ZH："AI 的发展速度超过了大多数人建立自信使用它的能力，而这个差距正是经过验证的评估所要弥合的。Anthropic 建立了 AI 实验室中首个有监考的认证体系，使用了与 Pearson 在专业执照考试中相同的可信基础设施来评估技能、颁发证书和验证成果。这再次证明了为什么 Anthropic 是全球企业值得信赖的 AI 合作伙伴。"

> EN: — Vishaal Gupta, President, Enterprise Learning and Skills, Pearson

ZH：—— Vishaal Gupta，Pearson 企业学习与技能总裁

> EN: Proctoring and identity verification matter because these credentials are used to make decisions. Certified practitioner counts determine a firm's standing in the Claude Partner Network and are relevant when companies vet who will work on their systems. A credential can only support those decisions if the assessment is rigorous and validates a person's applied skills.

ZH：监考和身份验证之所以重要，是因为这些证书将被用于做决策。认证从业者的数量决定了公司在 Claude Partner Network 中的等级，也与企业在考察谁将操作他们的系统时密切相关。只有评估过程严格、能够验证一个人的实际应用技能，证书才能真正支撑这些决策。

> EN: Our certification is gaining momentum inside the consulting and engineering firms implementing Claude for their clients. Certifications also determine how firms advance in the network. The top tier, Global Premier, requires 1,000 certified practitioners, 100 customers across three regions, and 15 public customer endorsements. The next tier review where this criteria will be evaluated across the network is on October 1.

ZH：我们的认证正在为客户实施 Claude 的咨询和工程公司中获得越来越多的认可。认证还决定了公司如何在该网络中晋升。最高级别 Global Premier 要求拥有 1,000 名认证从业者、覆盖三个地区的 100 个客户以及 15 个公开客户背书。下一次对全网进行这一标准评估的等级审核将在 10 月 1 日进行。
