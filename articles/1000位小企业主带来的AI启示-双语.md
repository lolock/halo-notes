# 一千位小企业主教会我们的 AI 课题 / What 1,000 small business owners taught us about AI

现在，大多数 AI 工具和培训都是为大型公司和有风险投资支持的初创企业打造的。小企业贡献了美国 GDP 的 44%，雇用了近一半的私营部门劳动力，却基本上只能独自摸索。作为一家公共利益公司，我们的部分职责是为 AI 创造更加公平的竞争环境；在这里，这意味着尽我们所能，让前沿 AI 抵达小企业和创业者的时间，与抵达《财富》500 强企业的时间保持同步。

因此，我们在 5 月推出了 Claude for Small Business：这是一个 Claude Cowork 插件，与 QuickBooks、PayPal、HubSpot、Canva、DocuSign 以及其他广受小企业欢迎的工具共同构建，能够将 Claude 连接到企业主已经在使用的工具上，并处理那些吞噬他们夜晚时间的后台工作。我们还与 PayPal 合作推出了新的《AI Fluency for Small Businesses》课程，专注于小企业主的工作流、工具以及更广泛的需求。

接下来的六周里，我们的团队与构建合作伙伴 Tenex 在芝加哥、塔尔萨、达拉斯、新泽西州汉密尔顿镇、巴吞鲁日、伯明翰、盐湖城、巴尔的摩、圣何塞和印第安纳波利斯举办了免费的半日工作坊。每一站都把 AI 流程素养培训与 Claude Cowork 实操结合起来：大约 100 位企业主打开笔记本电脑，在自己的真实业务中完成了一项自动化。共有超过 1,000 名中小企业主和运营者参加，我有幸听到了他们的故事。

他们分享了将 AI 引入工作的兴奋、挫折与希望：从帮助自动处理薪资，到消除那些让他们无法专注于工作中最喜欢部分的繁琐手工任务。大多数人来这里是为了学习一种新的 AI 素养方法，并掌握一条切实可用的 AI 工作流；许多人离开时还拥有了一个可以共同学习、试验这项强大新技术的同业社群。

以下就是他们教会我们的事情，以及我们接下来将如何推进这一项目。

<!-- lang:en -->

Most AI tools and training are built for large companies and venture-backed startups. Small businesses generate 44% of the U.S. GDP and employ close to half the private-sector workforce, and yet they have largely been left to figure it out alone. As a public benefit corporation, part of our job is leveling the playing field for AI, and in this case, that means doing our part to help frontier AI reach small business and entrepreneurs on the same timeline it reaches the Fortune 500.

So in May we launched Claude for Small Business: a Claude Cowork plugin, built alongside QuickBooks, PayPal, HubSpot, Canva, DocuSign, and more popular small business tools, that connects Claude to the tools owners already use and does the back-office work that eats up their evenings. We also released our new AI Fluency for Small Businesses course created with PayPal, focused on the workflows, tools, and broader needs of small business owners.

Over the following six weeks, our team and our build partner Tenex ran free, half-day workshops in Chicago, Tulsa, Dallas, Hamilton Township in New Jersey, Baton Rouge, Birmingham, Salt Lake City, Baltimore, San Jose, and Indianapolis. Each stop paired AI fluency training with a hands-on Claude Cowork session where roughly 100 business owners opened their laptops and automated something real in their business. More than 1,000 SMB owners and operators came through, and I had the fortune of hearing their stories.

They shared their excitement, frustrations, and hopes for bringing AI into their work, from helping automate payroll to removing the tedious, manual tasks that distract them from their favorite parts of their job. Most came to learn a new approach to AI fluency and pick up a tangible AI workflow, and many left with a community of peers in which to learn from and experiment with this powerful new technology.

Here is what they taught us and where we’re taking the program next.

## 教训一：AI 为非技术型创业者及其员工拉平了竞争环境 / Lesson 1: AI levels the playing field for non-technical entrepreneurs and their employees

<!-- bilingual:section -->

<!-- lang:zh -->

到场者是谁，与到场人数一样重要。注册者中有 80% 经营着拥有 5 至 50 名员工的公司，行业则偏向实体经济：建筑、制造、物流、各类技工行业，以及为这些行业提供服务的专业服务公司。一位依靠纸质排班表建立起拥有 100 名员工的商业清洁公司老板，带着正在接班的女儿一同参加活动，想一起实现排班自动化。自 1984 年起经营自己公司的芝加哥地区油漆承包商 Tony Severino，如今会让 Claude 按楼层读取 PDF 蓝图，帮助自己准备投标。在达拉斯郊外的一家工业系统集成商那里，现场技术人员只需拍下车间里没有标签的零件，Claude 几秒钟内就能识别出来；过去这一手工流程需要数小时。

最令我意外的现象是，许多没有软件背景的企业主和运营者，正在使用 AI 构建针对自身痛点的创意工具。

例如，印第安纳州 Liberty Trailers（一家拥有三座工厂的拖车制造商）的老板 Mike Teso，仅用大约 15 分钟就为一家新收购的工厂构建了一个对账工具；他的 IT 总监过去多年一直说自己不需要 AI，但后来仅用几天而不是几个月，就用实时看板取代了纸质生产排程。

尤其值得注意的是，那些在 AI 时代到来之前就创办企业的小企业主，使用 AI 的意愿非常强烈。他们把 AI 视为节省时间和资源的宝贵工具：既可以执行技术和运营工作，也能帮助他们把这些工作委派出去。许多人认为，AI 让他们获得了原本无法接触到的技能，从而赢得了先手优势。

<!-- lang:en -->

Who showed up mattered as much as how many. Eighty percent of registrants run companies with 5 to 50 employees, and the industries skewed toward the physical economy: construction, manufacturing, logistics, trades, and the professional services firms that serve them. A father who built a 100-person commercial cleaning company on paper schedules came with the daughter who is taking it over, to automate scheduling together. Tony Severino, a Chicago-area painting contractor who has run his own firm since 1984, now has Claude read PDF blueprints floor by floor to help him prep bids. At an industrial systems integrator outside Dallas, field technicians photograph an unlabeled part on the shop floor and Claude identifies it in seconds, a formerly manual process that used to take hours.

The pattern that surprised me most is how many owners and operators with no software background are using AI to build creative tooling specific to their pain points.

For example, Mike Teso, owner of Liberty Trailers, a three-plant trailer manufacturer in Indiana, built a reconciliation tool for a newly acquired factory in about 15 minutes; his IT director, who spent years saying he did not need AI, replaced paper production schedules with live dashboards in days rather than months.

In particular, small business owners who launched their businesses before the dawn of AI were eager to use it. They saw AI as a valuable tool for saving time and resources, both by doing technical and operational work and helping delegate it. Many viewed AI as offering a leg up by giving them skills they wouldn’t otherwise have access to.

## 教训二：企业主正用 AI 管理重要工作，但担心准确性 / Lesson 2: Owners are using AI to manage important work, but worry about accuracy

<!-- bilingual:section -->

<!-- lang:zh -->

当我们询问参与者希望 AI 完成什么时，他们描述的是一些最重要的运营工作：把三个系统中的数字汇总到周一报告中，或把今天下午的发现电话整理成一份新业务提案。大约三分之二的需求与经营业务有关，三分之一与业务增长有关；除营销之外，报告生成是最常见的单一使用场景。

当资源紧张时，AI 可以成为企业的平衡器。田纳西州一家只有 5 人的运输合规公司在市场低迷中失去了 60% 的客户，随后又收到核心软件供应商将在 30 天后终止服务的通知。团队借助 Claude 自行重建了系统，将燃油税申报错误率从 7% 降至零，如今仍由这 5 个人就能处理两倍于过去峰值的业务量。

不过，在这份兴奋之下，是一种普遍的担忧：重要事项会不会被遗漏。与 AI 协作是一种全新的工作方式，企业主经常谈到，他们需要学习如何验证 AI 的结果，而不是不加判断地照单全收。

企业主最认同的做法，是一开始把 Claude 当作一名新员工，随着时间推移逐步建立对其能力的信任和信心。我们在《AI Fluency》课程和工作坊现场都会提供具体指导，帮助大家培养辨别 AI 输出的能力。

因此，Broadcast Blinds 的老板 Rick Smith 现在会要求 Claude 标记哪些内容是它的推测、哪些是它知道的；Severino 也曾在一次早期投标中发现 Claude 使用了楼面面积乘数，而不是真实的墙面测量值。如今，他会要求 Claude “show me your work.”

随着模型逐渐了解他们日常工作的背景和组织需求，更多任务就可以委派给模型自主处理。

你可以在我们的《AI Fluency for Small Businesses》课程中深入学习这些概念。

<!-- lang:en -->

When we asked registrants what they wanted AI to do, they described some of the most important operational jobs: pull the numbers from three systems into the Monday report, or turn this afternoon's discovery call into a new business proposal. About two-thirds of these requests were about running the business and a third about growing it, and reporting was the single most common use case, outside of marketing work.

AI acts like an equalizer for businesses when resources are tight. A five-person trucking compliance shop in Tennessee lost 60% of its clients in a market downturn, then got 30 days' notice from its core software vendor. The team rebuilt the system themselves with Claude, took their fuel tax filing error rate from 7% to zero, and are now set up to handle twice their old peak volume with the same five people.

Still, underneath this excitement sat a common apprehension: something important falling through the cracks. Working with AI was a new motion, and business owners frequently spoke about the experience of learning how to validate its results and not take them at face value.

What resonated most with business owners was treating Claude like a new employee at first — building trust and confidence in its abilities over time. We give tactical guidance on how to build discernment in outputs both in our AI Fluency course and during the workshops themselves.

As a result, Rick Smith, owner of Broadcast Blinds, now prompts Claude to flag when it's assuming versus knowing, and Severino once caught Claude using a floor-area multiplier instead of real wall measurements on an early bid and now asks Claude to “show me your work.”

As the model gains context into their day-to-day workflows and organizational needs, more can be delegated to be handled autonomously.

You can learn these concepts in depth in our AI Fluency for Small Businesses course.

## 教训三：数据与治理是最受关注的问题 / Lesson 3: Data and governance top the list of concerns

<!-- bilingual:section -->

<!-- lang:zh -->

在我们对 503 名小企业决策者开展的上线前调查中，数据安全是最常被提及的 AI 采用障碍。在巡讲的每一站，大家首先提出的都是数据隐私、安全和治理问题：我的数据会被用于训练你们的模型吗？如果我连接 QuickBooks，Claude 具体能看到什么？如果代理替我浏览网页，恶意页面能否劫持它？

企业主从供应商、顾问和新闻标题中听到彼此矛盾的信息，因此希望有一个用通俗语言写成的事实依据。这种困惑是有代价的：根据我们的产品数据，那些有足够信心连接自己工具的企业主获得的价值要高得多；而一旦产生疑虑，采用就会停滞。

在 AI 采用进程中走得最远的企业主，往往通过有意让人始终掌握主导权，亲自回答了信任问题。在印第安纳波利斯一家由女性主导、拥有 40 名员工的工程公司 KBSO Consulting，新员工会按设计要求审查每一份 Claude 生成的摘要，并将此作为在岗培训的一部分。新泽西一家夫妻经营的品牌代理公司自动化了从提案到合同的整个流程，但在每封潜在客户开发邮件上都保留人工点击 “send” 的步骤。达拉斯一家拥有 20 名员工、负责处理客户薪资数据的后台服务公司 HireEffect，则在向员工推广之前建立了个人身份信息（PII）脱敏机制，并登记了每一条 Claude 工作流。

很明显，我们还需要在信任和安全教育方面做更多工作。

你可以控制自己的对话是否被用于改进 Claude；在 Team 和 Enterprise 套餐中，我们默认不会使用这些对话进行训练。你现有的权限会继续有效（如果员工今天在 QuickBooks 中看不到某项内容，他们也无法通过 Claude 看到它），而我们会在产品和模型中构建强大的安全性与防护措施。你可以在这里和这里找到更多信息。

<!-- lang:en -->

In our pre-launch survey of 503 small business decision-makers, data security was the most-cited barrier to AI adoption. At every tour stop, questions about data privacy, security, and governance were the first to be asked: Is my data used to train your models? If I connect QuickBooks, what exactly can Claude see? If an agent browses the web for me, can a malicious page hijack it?

Owners hear conflicting things from vendors, consultants, and headlines, and they want one plain-language source of truth. This confusion has a cost: in our product data, owners who feel confident enough to connect their tools get far more value, and when there’s doubt, adoption stalls.

The business owners furthest along in their AI adoption journey have answered the trust question for themselves by deliberately keeping a person in the driver's seat. At KBSO Consulting, a women-led, 40-person engineering firm in Indianapolis, new hires review every Claude-generated summary, by design, as on-the-job training. A husband-and-wife branding agency in New Jersey automated their entire proposal-to-contract flow but kept a human "send" on every prospecting email. And HireEffect, a 20-person back-office firm in Dallas that handles clients' payroll data, built PII redaction and a registry of every Claude workflow before rolling it out to staff.

It was obvious there was more to do on our end to educate about trust and security.

You control whether your conversations are used to improve Claude, and on Team and Enterprise plans we don't train on them by default. Your existing permissions carry over (if an employee can't see it in QuickBooks today, they can't see it through Claude), and we build strong security and safeguards into our product and our models. ## 教训四：搭建 Agent 对初学者可能令人生畏，但开启第一条工作流后就不同了 / Lesson 4: Building agents can be intimidating–until you kick off the first workflow

<!-- bilingual:section -->

<!-- lang:zh -->

这次巡讲让我看到了 AI 采用的另一个障碍：AI 素养，也就是理解并善用 AI 的能力。在项目启动前，我们调查的 81% 小企业主表示愿意尝试新的 AI 工具；他们最主要的痛点，是员工不确定该如何或何时使用这些工具。

巡讲证实了这一点：许多参与者每天已经在使用 Claude chat，却尚未尝试 Cowork、skills 或 connectors。Quentin Durr 在结束 18 年银行业生涯后，全情投入自己共同创办的增长系统公司。他坦率地告诉我们：“我使用 AI 模型已经很多年了，但直到 skills 和 connectors 出现，我才在实际工作中找到真正的使用场景。”其他人则告诉我们，功能更新的速度快得让他们跟不上，为团队编写的任何培训材料几乎立刻就会过时。

真正弥合差距的，是有人在身边提供帮助时亲自花时间操作键盘。具体来说，一旦小企业主构建出自己的第一条工作流，他们就会获得继续构建的信心，进而部署到生产环境中的 Agent。在 635 份离场调查中，最常被提及的亮点是引导式构建环节，最常见的请求则是希望增加这类环节。近三分之二的参与者希望在工作坊结束后获得实施 AI 的实操帮助。

对许多人而言，开始使用 Claude Cowork 是最困难的一步；它是一种全新的工作方式，将 AI 和 Agents 融入 Microsoft 365、PayPal、Intuit Quickbooks 等常见的小企业工具中。就像掌握任何新工具一样，它通常都有陡峭的学习曲线。不过，一旦他们真正设置好 Claude Cowork 并实施了第一条工作流——例如构建虚拟幕僚长或自动处理薪资——影响几乎立刻就会显现。

在最后一站，一家拥有 30 名员工的制造企业 CEO 告诉我：“你们帮我完成了那些我原本害怕去做的事情。”对大多数人来说，只需几个小时的引导式练习，就能掌握如何让 AI 的应用超越聊天机器人，真正运行多步骤任务。

为了帮助你开始，我们建议安装 Claude for Small Business（任何套餐均可使用），然后让 Claude “help me get onboarded onto the small business plug-in.”

<!-- lang:en -->

The tour opened my eyes to another barrier to AI adoption: AI fluency, in other words, the ability to understand and use AI well. Before launch, 81% of small business owners we surveyed said they were open to new AI tools, and their top pain point was employees being unsure how or when to use them.

The tour confirmed it: many attendees already used Claude chat daily but hadn't tried Cowork, skills, or connectors. Quentin Durr, who left an 18-year banking career to go all in at the growth systems company he co-founded, told us plainly: “I’ve used AI models for years, but it wasn’t until skills and connectors came along that I found real use cases in my actual work.” Others told us features arrive faster than they can track, and that any training they write for their team goes stale almost immediately.

What closed the gap was time on the keyboard with someone nearby to help; specifically, once a small business owner built their first workflow, they felt empowered to keep building, and in turn, deploy production agents. Across 635 exit surveys, the most-cited highlight was the guided build session, and the most common request was more of it. Nearly two-thirds of attendees asked for hands-on help implementing AI after the workshop.

To many, getting started with Claude Cowork was the hardest part; it’s a new way of working that incorporates AI and Agents into common small business tools, like Microsoft 365, PayPal, Intuit Quickbooks, and more, and like picking up any new tool, there is often a steep learning curve. Still, once they actually set up Claude Cowork and implemented their first workflow (i.e., building a virtual chief-of-staff or automating payroll), the impact was near instant.

At our last stop, the CEO of a 30-person manufacturer told me, "You were able to help me do the things I was scared of doing" with AI. For most, a few hours of guided practice was all it took to get the hang of taking AI adoption beyond using a chatbot and actually running multi-step tasks.

To help get you started, we recommend installing Claude for Small Business (available on any plan!) and asking Claude to “help me get onboarded onto the small business plug-in.”

## 教训五：小企业主最希望向同伴学习 / Lesson 5: Small business owners want to learn from their peers

<!-- bilingual:section -->

<!-- lang:zh -->

这次巡讲的每个会场，都是由当地合作伙伴组织起来的。商会、地区 AI 中心和经济发展机构邀请了各自的成员。

在构建环节之后，离场调查中最常被提及的亮点，是听其他企业主分享他们如何解决同一个问题；与会者提出建立同伴使用案例共享库的次数，也超过了对任何产品功能的请求。企业主们按行业组队、交换联系方式，并索要参会者名单，以便继续交流和比较经验。

大约五分之一的参与者已经是为其他小企业提供教学的顾问或教育者，活动后又有几位企业主成为了这样的角色。例如，Pat Miller 运营着 Small Business Owners Community，这是一个覆盖全国的单人创业者社群；他还主持一档每日播客，并表示，如果没有 Claude Cowork 负责制作，这档播客不可能存在。如今，他也在向其他单人创始人教授 Claude 的用法。

在伯明翰工作坊之后，35 人规模的网络公司 Infomedia 的高级副总裁 Carrie Rollwagen 开发了一套两小时的内部 Claude 培训。该培训已经举办了四轮，并帮助员工从行政工作转向创意岗位，同时没有缩减团队规模。

对小企业主而言，最有效的老师，是另一位正在使用 AI 应对相似挑战的小企业主。

<!-- lang:en -->

Every room on this tour was filled by a local partner. Chambers of commerce, regional AI hubs, and economic development organizations invited their members.

After the build session, the most-cited highlight in the exit surveys was hearing how other owners had solved the same problem, and attendees asked for a shared repository of peer use cases more often than any product feature. Owners grouped up by industry, traded numbers, and asked for the attendee list so they could keep comparing notes.

Roughly one in five attendees was already a consultant or educator teaching other small businesses, and several owners have become one since. For instance, Pat Miller runs Small Business Owners Community, a nationwide solopreneur group and a daily podcast he says could not exist without Claude Cowork handling production; he now teaches Claude to other solo founders.

After our Birmingham workshop, Carrie Rollwagen, an SVP at the 35-person web firm Infomedia, built a two-hour internal Claude training that has filled four times and moved staff from administrative work into creative roles without shrinking the team.

The most effective teacher for a small business owner is another small business owner using AI to tackle similar challenges.
