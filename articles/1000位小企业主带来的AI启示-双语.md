# 一千位小企业主教会我们的 AI 课题 / What 1,000 small business owners taught us about AI

> EN: Most AI tools and training are built for large companies and venture-backed startups. Small businesses generate 44% of the U.S. GDP and employ close to half the private-sector workforce, and yet they have largely been left to figure it out alone. As a public benefit corporation, part of our job is leveling the playing field for AI, and in this case, that means doing our part to help frontier AI reach small business and entrepreneurs on the same timeline it reaches the Fortune 500.
>
> ZH: 现在大多数 AI 工具和培训都主要为大型公司和风投背景初创企业设计。小企业贡献了美国 44% 的 GDP，并吸纳了接近一半的私营部门劳动力，却长期被迫自己摸索。作为一家公共利益公司，我们的一部分职责就是尽量拉平 AI 的起点差距，让前沿 AI 在到达《财富 500》时，也能同步到达小企业和创业者。

> EN: So in May we launched Claude for Small Business: a Claude Cowork plugin, built alongside QuickBooks, PayPal, HubSpot, Canva, DocuSign, and more popular small business tools, that connects Claude to the tools owners already use and does the back-office work that eats up their evenings. We also released our new AI Fluency for Small Businesses course created with PayPal, focused on the workflows, tools, and broader needs of small business owners.
>
> ZH: 因此我们在 5 月推出了 Claude for Small Business。这是一个面向小企业的 Claude Cowork 插件，已与 QuickBooks、PayPal、HubSpot、Canva、DocuSign 等常用工具打通，可直接连接企业主日常使用的软件，把大量夜间占用时间的后台事务交给 AI 执行。我们还与 PayPal 合作上线了《AI Fluency for Small Businesses》课程，聚焦小企业主的工作流、工具链和实际痛点。

> EN: Over the following six weeks, our team and our build partner Tenex ran free, half-day workshops in Chicago, Tulsa, Dallas, Hamilton Township in New Jersey, Baton Rouge, Birmingham, Salt Lake City, Baltimore, San Jose, and Indianapolis. Each stop paired AI fluency training with a hands-on Claude Cowork session where roughly 100 business owners opened their laptops and automated something real in their business. More than 1,000 SMB owners and operators came through, and I had the fortune of hearing their stories.
>
> ZH: 此后六周里，团队与合作伙伴 Tenex 在芝加哥、塔尔萨、达拉斯、新泽西州汉密尔顿镇、巴吞鲁日、伯明翰、盐湖城、巴尔的摩、圣何塞和印第安纳波利斯举办了多场免费的半日工作坊。每站都将 AI 流程素养培训与 Claude Cowork 实操课结合，约 100 位企业主现场上手在真实业务中自动化一次具体任务。超过 1,000 名 SMB 主与运营者参与，我有幸听到了他们的真实故事。

> EN: They shared their excitement, frustrations, and hopes for bringing AI into their work, from helping automate payroll to removing the tedious, manual tasks that distract them from their favorite parts of their job. Most came to learn a new approach to AI fluency and pick up a tangible AI workflow, and many left with a community of peers in which to learn from and experiment with this powerful new technology.
>
> ZH: 他们表达了对 AI 的期待、困惑和顾虑，既想自动化发薪等重复工作，也希望去掉耗时琐碎的手工流程，让自己把精力放回到更有价值的环节。多数人来这里是为了学习一套可落地的 AI 使用方法并拿到可操作的工作流，很多人结束后还带着新的同业圈子和同伴一起持续实验。

> EN: Here is what they taught us and where we’re taking the program next.
>
> ZH: 他们带来的核心教训是这些，也决定了我们后续的推进方向。

## 教训一：AI 为非技术型创业者及其团队拉平了起跑线 / Lesson 1: AI levels the playing field for non-technical entrepreneurs and their employees

> EN: Who showed up mattered as much as how many. Eighty percent of registrants run companies with 5 to 50 employees, and the industries skewed toward the physical economy: construction, manufacturing, logistics, trades, and the professional services firms that serve them. A father who built a 100-person commercial cleaning company on paper schedules came with the daughter who is taking it over, to automate scheduling together. Tony Severino, a Chicago-area painting contractor who has run his own firm since 1984, now has Claude read PDF blueprints floor by floor to help him prep bids. At an industrial systems integrator outside Dallas, field technicians photograph an unlabeled part on the shop floor and Claude identifies it in seconds, a formerly manual process that used to take hours.
>
> ZH: 到场的人数当然重要，但更关键的是谁来了。注册者中，80% 经营的是 5 到 50 人的小型公司，行业集中在实体经济领域：建筑、制造、物流、行业工种，以及为这些行业服务的专业服务机构。一个从纸质排班起步、建立起 100 人商业清洁团队的企业家，带着接班的女儿一起现场参与，希望把排班自动化。芝加哥地区画家承包商 Tony Severino（自 1984 年起经营自己的公司）现在会让 Claude 按楼层逐页读取 PDF 施工图，协助整理投标资料；而在达拉斯附近的一家工业系统集成商，现场技师拍下未标记零件后，Claude 在几秒内即可识别过去要花数小时人工判断的内容。

> EN: The pattern that surprised me most is how many owners and operators with no software background are using AI to build creative tooling specific to their pain points.
>
> ZH: 让我最意外的是：大量没有软件开发背景的企业主和管理者，也在用 AI 反向构建“定制工具”，把 AI 当作解决具体业务痛点的生产力底座。

> EN: For example, Mike Teso, owner of Liberty Trailers, a three-plant trailer manufacturer in Indiana, built a reconciliation tool for a newly acquired factory in about 15 minutes; his IT director, who spent years saying he did not need AI, replaced paper production schedules with live dashboards in days rather than months.
>
> ZH: 比如，印第安纳州 Liberty Trailers（一家三厂房拖车制造商）负责人 Mike Teso 在接手一家新工厂时，用约 15 分钟就搭起了对账工具；他的 IT 负责人曾长期认为不需要 AI，但后来却在几天内把纸质生产排程改成了实时看板，周期从“几个月”缩短为“几天”。

> EN: In particular, small business owners who launched their businesses before the dawn of AI were eager to use it. They saw AI as a valuable tool for saving time and resources, both by doing technical and operational work and helping delegate it. Many viewed AI as offering a leg up by giving them skills they wouldn’t otherwise have access to.
>
> ZH: 尤其是那些在 AI 时代来临前就创业的老板，反而更积极尝试。他们把 AI 视作节约时间和资源的实用工具，既能帮助完成技术/运营动作，也能辅助任务分配。对许多人来说，AI 提供了过去难以触达的能力——这在短期内就形成了明显竞争优势。

## 教训二：企业主用于关键工作的 AI 需求上升，但担心结果准确性 / Lesson 2: Owners are using AI to manage important work, but worry about accuracy

> EN: When we asked registrants what they wanted AI to do, they described some of the most important operational jobs: pull the numbers from three systems into the Monday report, or turn this afternoon's discovery call into a new business proposal. About two-thirds of these requests were about running the business and a third about growing it, and reporting was the single most common use case, outside of marketing work.
>
> ZH: 当我们询问参与者希望 AI 做什么时，很多需求都指向核心运营动作，比如把三个系统的数据汇入周一报表，或把当日下午的 discovery call 直接转成新的业务提案。大约三分之二的问题属于“维持业务运转”，三分之一偏“业务增长”，在营销之外，报表自动化是单独提及最频繁的场景。

> EN: AI acts like an equalizer for businesses when resources are tight. A five-person trucking compliance shop in Tennessee lost 60% of its clients in a market downturn, then got 30 days' notice from its core software vendor. The team rebuilt the system themselves with Claude, took their fuel tax filing error rate from 7% to zero, and are now set up to handle twice their old peak volume with the same five people.
>
> ZH: 在资源紧缩时，AI 更像一层“平衡器”。田纳西州一家 5 人的运输合规工作室在市场下行中流失了 60% 客户，又在核心软件供应商处拿到 30 天通知期。团队借助 Claude 自己重建了系统，将燃油税申报错误率从 7% 降到 0，并且现在 5 人也能处理过去峰值两倍的业务量。

> EN: Still, underneath this excitement sat a common apprehension: something important falling through the cracks. Working with AI was a new motion, and business owners frequently spoke about the experience of learning how to validate its results and not take them at face value.
>
> ZH: 这种热情背后仍有共同担心：关键环节会不会掉链子。AI 对他们来说是新的工作方式，很多企业主反复提到的关键学习点，是如何验证 AI 的输出、而不是把它当作结论直接采信。

> EN: What resonated most with business owners was treating Claude like a new employee at first — building trust and confidence in its abilities over time. We give tactical guidance on how to build discernment in outputs both in our AI Fluency course and during the workshops themselves.
>
> ZH: 与许多企业主沟通后，最有共鸣的观点是：Claude 一开始应先像一名新同事被试用，逐步建立信任和判断力。我们在《AI Fluency》课程和现场工作坊中，都强调如何训练“输出审查机制”，让结果通过人来核验。

> EN: As a result, Rick Smith, owner of Broadcast Blinds, now prompts Claude to flag when it's assuming versus knowing, and Severino once caught Claude using a floor-area multiplier instead of real wall measurements on an early bid and now asks Claude to “show me your work.”
>
> ZH: 因此，例如 Broadcast Blinds 的创始人 Rick Smith 会要求 Claude 区分“推测”与“已确认”的信息；Tony Severino 也曾在早期投标中发现 Claude 使用了楼层面积系数而非真实墙体测量数据，于是后续他会要求 Claude “展示你是怎么得出这个结论的”。

> EN: As the model gains context into their day-to-day workflows and organizational needs, more can be delegated to be handled autonomously.
>
> ZH: 当模型逐步吸收企业日常流程和组织边界后，越多的环节可以交由系统更自动地完成。

> EN: You can learn these concepts in depth in our AI Fluency for Small Businesses course.
>
> ZH: 这些“用量化方式建立审慎使用习惯”的方法，已经在《AI Fluency for Small Businesses》课程中做了完整展开。

## 教训三：数据与治理是最核心关注点 / Lesson 3: Data and governance top the list of concerns

> EN: In our pre-launch survey of 503 small business decision-makers, data security was the most-cited barrier to AI adoption. At every tour stop, questions about data privacy, security, and governance were the first to be asked: Is my data used to train your models? If I connect QuickBooks, what exactly can Claude see? If an agent browses the web for me, can a malicious page hijack it?
>
> ZH: 在我们对 503 名小企业决策者的上线前调研中，数据安全是最常被提及的 AI 落地阻力。每一站的讨论一开始，都会优先出现数据隐私、安全和治理问题：我的数据会用于训练模型吗？连接 QuickBooks 后 Claude 能看到哪些内容？如果让代理去浏览网页，是否会被恶意网页劫持？

> EN: Owners hear conflicting things from vendors, consultants, and headlines, and they want one plain-language source of truth. This confusion has a cost: in our product data, owners who feel confident enough to connect their tools get far more value, and when there’s doubt, adoption stalls.
>
> ZH: 企业主从供应商、咨询方和媒体里听到的信息往往不一致，他们希望有一个清晰透明的“权威说明书”。这种不确定直接影响采纳效果：在我们的产品数据中，愿意并理解地连接工具的企业主能获得明显更高的价值；有疑虑者则往往停滞不前。

> EN: The business owners furthest along in their AI adoption journey have answered the trust question for themselves by deliberately keeping a person in the driver's seat. At KBSO Consulting, a women-led, 40-person engineering firm in Indianapolis, new hires review every Claude-generated summary, by design, as on-the-job training. A husband-and-wife branding agency in New Jersey automated their entire proposal-to-contract flow but kept a human "send" on every prospecting email. And HireEffect, a 20-person back-office firm in Dallas that handles clients' payroll data, built PII redaction and a registry of every Claude workflow before rolling it out to staff.
>
> ZH: 在 AI 采用度较高的企业里，负责人往往会在组织内保留“人主导环节”。印第安纳波利斯一家 40 人、女性主导的 KBSO Consulting 要求新员工将每份 Claude 生成摘要都做人工复核，作为在岗训练的一部分；新泽西的一家夫妻合伙品牌公司把提案到合同的全流程都自动化，但每封拓展邮件仍保留人工发送环节；达拉斯一家 20 人的后台公司 HireEffect 在推广给员工前，先建立了 PII 脱敏和全部 Claude 工作流清单制度。

> EN: It was obvious there was more to do on our end to educate about trust and security.
>
> ZH: 由此可见，我们还有大量工作要做，尤其是在“信任与安全”教育与说明上。

> EN: You control whether your conversations are used to improve Claude, and on Team and Enterprise plans we don't train on them by default. Your existing permissions carry over (if an employee can't see it in QuickBooks today, they can't see it through Claude), and we build strong security and safeguards into our product and our models. You can find more information here and here.
>
> ZH: 你可以控制对话是否用于改进 Claude，并且在 Team 与 Enterprise 套餐中默认不拿你的会话进行训练。现有权限体系会完整沿用（员工在 QuickBooks 看不到的内容，Claude 也不会看到）。我们在产品与模型层都做了较强的安全和防护设计。更多细节可参见官方安全说明。

## 教训四：搭建 Agent 对初学者有门槛，但第一条工作流是关键 / Lesson 4: Building agents can be intimidating–until you kick off the first workflow

> EN: The tour opened my eyes to another barrier to AI adoption: AI fluency, in other words, the ability to understand and use AI well. Before launch, 81% of small business owners we surveyed said they were open to new AI tools, and their top pain point was employees being unsure how or when to use them.
>
> ZH: 这次巡讲让我看到另一个阻力：AI 流畅度问题——也就是能否真正理解并正确使用 AI。在正式发布前的调研中，81% 的小企业主表示愿意尝试新的 AI 工具，但最突出的痛点是员工不知道该在什么场景用、怎样用。

> EN: The tour confirmed it: many attendees already used Claude chat daily but hadn't tried Cowork, skills, or connectors. Quentin Durr, who left an 18-year banking career to go all in at the growth systems company he co-founded, told us plainly: “I’ve used AI models for years, but it wasn’t until skills and connectors came along that I found real use cases in my actual work.” Others told us features arrive faster than they can track, and that any training they write for their team goes stale almost immediately.
>
> ZH: 事实也印证了这一点：许多参与者虽然每天都在用 Claude chat，但并未真正使用 Cowork、skills 或 connectors。与我们同机交流的 Quentin Durr（从事银行 18 年后加入自己共同创办的增长系统公司）直言："我用了 AI 模型很多年，但直到有了 skills 与 connectors，我才发现它在真实工作里有了可落地的用例。" 也有人反映，平台新功能更迭很快，团队培训内容很快就会过期。

> EN: What closed the gap was time on the keyboard with someone nearby to help; specifically, once a small business owner built their first workflow, they felt empowered to keep building, and in turn, deploy production agents. Across 635 exit surveys, the most-cited highlight was the guided build session, and the most common request was more of it. Nearly two-thirds of attendees asked for hands-on help implementing AI after the workshop.
>
> ZH: 真正填补这道鸿沟的，是“有伴随、有指导的上手时长”。一旦小企业主完成第一个工作流，接下来就更有意愿继续搭建并部署生产级 Agent。在 635 份回访问卷中，最常被点名的亮点是“引导式搭建环节”，最常见的反馈也是“希望再多来一次”；近三分之二的参与者在工作坊后都申请了继续的落地辅导。

> EN: To many, getting started with Claude Cowork was the hardest part; it’s a new way of working that incorporates AI and Agents into common small business tools, like Microsoft 365, PayPal, Intuit Quickbooks, and more, and like picking up any new tool, there is often a steep learning curve. Still, once they actually set up Claude Cowork and implemented their first workflow (i.e., building a virtual chief-of-staff or automating payroll), the impact was near instant.
>
> ZH: 对很多人而言，Claude Cowork 的入门最困难，因为它把 AI 与 Agents 嵌入了常见业务工具（如 Microsoft 365、PayPal、Intuit QuickBooks 等），学习曲线自然不低。不过，一旦真正完成第一次配置并跑通第一条工作流——无论是搭建虚拟行政助理，还是自动化发薪流程——效果几乎是即刻显现的。

> EN: At our last stop, the CEO of a 30-person manufacturer told me, "You were able to help me do the things I was scared of doing" with AI. For most, a few hours of guided practice was all it took to get the hang of taking AI adoption beyond using a chatbot and actually running multi-step tasks.
>
> ZH: 我在最后一站听到一家 30 人制造企业的 CEO 说："是你们帮我把一些我不敢动的事情做了。" 对多数企业主而言，仅用几小时的指导实操，就能完成从“会用聊天界面”到“能运行多步任务”的跃迁。

> EN: To help get you started, we recommend installing Claude for Small Business (available on any plan!) and asking Claude to “help me get onboarded onto the small business plug-in.”
>
> ZH: 想快速起步的话，官方建议先安装 Claude for Small Business（任何套餐都可使用），并让 Claude 引导自己完成小企业插件的接入。

## 教训五：小企业最想从同伴那里学 / Lesson 5: Small business owners want to learn from their peers

> EN: Every room on this tour was filled by a local partner. Chambers of commerce, regional AI hubs, and economic development organizations invited their members.
>
> ZH: 每场巡讲都依托当地合作方完成。商会、地区 AI 中心及经济发展机构都参与组织，把本地成员组织起来。

> EN: After the build session, the most-cited highlight in the exit surveys was hearing how other owners had solved the same problem, and attendees asked for a shared repository of peer use cases more often than any product feature. Owners grouped up by industry, traded numbers, and asked for the attendee list so they could keep comparing notes.
>
> ZH: 在现场搭建之后，回访里反复出现的高频反馈是“想听别的老板如何解决同类问题”。与会者比起功能性产品特性，更频繁地要求建立同业案例库。大家按行业分组交流数据、复盘方案，还会主动留存参会名单，持续比对经验。

> EN: Roughly one in five attendees was already a consultant or educator teaching other small businesses, and several owners have become one since. For instance, Pat Miller runs Small Business Owners Community, a nationwide solopreneur group and a daily podcast he says could not exist without Claude Cowork handling production; he now teaches Claude to other solo founders.
>
> ZH: 大约五分之一的参与者本身就是咨询师或培训者，帮助其他小企业；活动后又有不少人因此转为这类角色。比如 Pat Miller 运营的 Small Business Owners Community（全国独立创业者社群）以及其日更播客，都高度依赖 Claude Cowork 的内容生产流程，他也开始将 Claude 经验反向教给更多单干创业者。

> EN: After our Birmingham workshop, Carrie Rollwagen, an SVP at the 35-person web firm Infomedia, built a two-hour internal Claude training that has filled four times and moved staff from administrative work into creative roles without shrinking the team.
>
> ZH: 伯明翰站结束后，Infomedia（一家 35 人的网络公司）副总裁 Carrie Rollwagen 做了两小时的内部 Claude 培训课程，这门课目前已开设四期，帮助团队将部分同事从纯行政事务转向更具创造性的岗位，而没有压缩团队规模。

> EN: The most effective teacher for a small business owner is another small business owner using AI to tackle similar challenges.
>
> ZH: 对小企业主而言，最有说服力的老师，往往不是供应商，而是另一位正在用 AI 解决同类问题的创业者。
