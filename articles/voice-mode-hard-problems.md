> EN: Starting today, voice mode runs on Claude Opus, Claude Sonnet, and Claude Haiku, reaches the tools you've connected like Gmail and Slack, and speaks many more languages.

> EN: Some problems you can't type your way through. Voice mode is for practicing for an important pitch meeting, deciding between multiple offers, reviewing your own process out loud, or brainstorming new ideas.

ZH: 有些问题你无法靠打字解决。语音模式适合用来演练重要的 pitch 会议、在多个 offer 之间做决策、大声复盘自己的工作流程，或者头脑风暴新想法。

## 一个能跟上你节奏的"思考陪练" / A sounding board that keeps up

> EN: After we added hands-free conversation earlier this year, people started using voice mode for more than quick questions. They began working through real business problems across longer sessions, out loud rather than through text. But until now, voice mode only ran on our Claude Haiku model, which we chose for speed. It kept conversations quick, but not always deep.

ZH: 今年早些时候我们加入免提对话功能后，人们开始把语音模式用在更多场景——不仅仅是快速提问。他们在更长的会话中用语音模式来处理真实的商业问题，用说而不是写的方式来思考。但到目前为止，语音模式只能在 Claude Haiku 上运行，选择 Haiku 是因为它的速度够快。这让对话保持敏捷，但深度有时不够。

> EN: Claude Opus and Sonnet, models designed for hard problem-solving, are now available in voice mode. You can switch models mid-conversation from the model picker. Voice mode uses the fastest version of whichever model you've selected, so the conversation runs smoothly. It defaults to the last model you used in text chat, so you can move between voice and text without starting over.

ZH: Claude Opus 和 Sonnet——这两款专为攻克难题设计的模型——现在也支持语音模式了。你可以在对话过程中通过模型选择器切换模型。语音模式会使用你当前所选模型的最快版本，让对话流畅进行。它默认沿用你在文字聊天中最后使用的模型，所以你可以无缝地在语音和文字之间切换，无需重新开始。

> EN: With more capable models, you can talk through a half-formed idea and work out what you actually think. Claude asks follow-up questions and builds on your thinking rather than handing you an answer. Voice mode takes turns, meaning Claude listens, pauses to think, and then responds.

ZH: 有了更强的模型，你可以把半成型的想法说出来，然后在对话中理清自己到底是怎么想的。Claude 会追问、在你思考的基础上延伸，而不是直接丢给你一个答案。语音模式是轮流对话——Claude 先听，停下来思考，然后再回应。

> EN: A few things to try:

ZH: 可以试试这些：

- Practice for an important conversation and ask Claude for feedback on your communication style.
  ZH: 演练一场重要对话，让 Claude 给你的沟通风格提反馈。
- Talk through a client pitch and ask Claude to find gaps in your logic.
  ZH: 口头过一遍客户 pitch，让 Claude 帮你找逻辑漏洞。
- Brainstorm ideas for your product roadmap and ask Claude to validate them with competitive research.
  ZH: 头脑风暴产品路线图，让 Claude 用竞品调研来验证你的想法。
- Ask Claude to help you evaluate a few different hypotheses for why your last video went viral.
  ZH: 让 Claude 帮你评估几个假设，分析为什么你上一个视频火了。

## 从说到做 / From talking to doing

> EN: When you've decided what to do, ask Claude to do it. Some examples of what you can achieve in your tools:

ZH: 当你决定了要做什么，就让 Claude 去执行。以下是一些你可以通过已连接工具完成的事：

- Running late? Ask Claude to push a meeting on your Google Calendar by 30 minutes.
  ZH: 要迟到了？让 Claude 把你的 Google Calendar 会议推迟 30 分钟。
- Turn a conversation about a client pitch into a one-pager in Canva.
  ZH: 把一场关于客户 pitch 的对话直接变成 Canva 里的一页纸文档。
- Ask Claude to summarize the emails you've received today and draft responses to the most critical ones.
  ZH: 让 Claude 总结你今天收到的邮件，并为最关键的几封起草回复。

> EN: Claude will ask for permission before using one of your connected tools. You can connect a new tool in Settings > Connectors on the Claude mobile, desktop or web apps.

ZH: Claude 在使用你已连接的工具前会先征得你的许可。你可以在 Claude 移动端、桌面端或网页版的 Settings > Connectors 中连接新工具。

## 用更多语言对话 / Converse in many more languages

> EN: With many more languages now available in voice mode on every plan, you can talk with Claude in the language you think in, switching mid-conversation. Available languages include:

ZH: 现在所有套餐的语音模式都支持更多语言，你可以用自己最习惯的语言和 Claude 对话，还能在对话中随时切换。支持的语言包括：

- English（英语）
- French（法语）
- German（德语）
- Hindi（印地语）
- Indonesian（印尼语）
- Italian（意大利语）
- Japanese（日语）
- Korean（韩语）
- Portuguese (Brazilian)（巴西葡萄牙语）
- Spanish (Latin America)（拉美西班牙语）
- Spanish (Spain)（西班牙西班牙语）

> EN: Tell Claude out loud to switch from English to your language of choice, or select your language from the language picker in voice settings.

ZH: 直接口头告诉 Claude 从英语切换到你选择的语言，或者在语音设置的语言选择器中选择。

> EN: Claude doesn't automatically detect your language, so you'll need to ask out loud or select your language to make the switch from English. You will need to set your language specifically for voice mode, as your previous language settings will not carry over.

ZH: Claude 不会自动检测你的语言，所以你需要口头要求切换，或者在设置中选择语言来从英语切换。你需要专门为语音模式设置语言，因为之前的语言设置不会自动继承到语音模式。

## 如何开始 / Getting started

> EN: The latest update to voice mode is available in beta to all chat users on mobile, desktop, and web, but works best from your phone. On the Free plan, you get Claude Haiku, one connected tool, and all available languages. Paid plans can access expanded models and all your connected tools. Voice conversations count toward your regular usage limits.

ZH: 最新版语音模式以 beta 形式向所有聊天用户开放，覆盖移动端、桌面端和网页版，但在手机上体验最佳。Free 套餐用户可使用 Claude Haiku、一个已连接工具和所有支持的语言。付费套餐用户可以使用更多模型和全部已连接工具。语音对话计入常规使用配额。

> EN: Try voice mode in the Claude mobile app by tapping the sound wave icon, or download the app to get started.

ZH: 在 Claude 移动端 app 中点击声波图标即可试用语音模式，或者下载 app 开始体验。
