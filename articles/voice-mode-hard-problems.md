Starting today, voice mode runs on Claude Opus, Claude Sonnet, and Claude Haiku, reaches the tools you've connected like Gmail and Slack, and speaks many more languages.

Some problems you can't type your way through. Voice mode is for practicing for an important pitch meeting, deciding between multiple offers, reviewing your own process out loud, or brainstorming new ideas.

<!-- /bilingual:section -->

## 一个能跟上你节奏的“思考陪练” / A sounding board that keeps up

<!-- bilingual:section -->

<!-- lang:zh -->

今年早些时候加入免提对话功能后，人们开始将语音模式用于更多场景，而不只是快速提问。他们在更长的会话中，通过口头表达而不是文字，逐步解决真实的商业问题。但直到现在，语音模式一直只能运行于 Claude Haiku——我们当初选择它，是因为它速度快。这样虽然能让对话保持敏捷，却不总能保证足够的深度。

<!-- lang:en -->

After we added hands-free conversation earlier this year, people started using voice mode for more than quick questions. They began working through real business problems across longer sessions, out loud rather than through text. But until now, voice mode only ran on our Claude Haiku model, which we chose for speed. It kept conversations quick, but not always deep.

<!-- /bilingual:section -->

<!-- bilingual:section -->

<!-- lang:zh -->

现在，专为解决复杂问题而设计的 Claude Opus 和 Sonnet 也可用于语音模式。你可以在会话过程中通过模型选择器切换模型。语音模式会使用你所选模型的最快版本，让对话流畅进行。它默认使用你在文字聊天中最近使用的模型，因此你可以在语音与文字之间切换，而无需重新开始。

借助能力更强的模型，你可以说出一个尚未成形的想法，并在交流中理清自己真正的想法。Claude 会提出后续问题，在你的思路上继续推进，而不是直接给出答案。语音模式采用轮流对话的方式：Claude 会先倾听，停下来思考，然后再作出回应。

<!-- lang:en -->

Claude Opus and Sonnet, models designed for hard problem-solving, are now available in voice mode. You can switch models mid-conversation from the model picker. Voice mode uses the fastest version of whichever model you've selected, so the conversation runs smoothly. It defaults to the last model you used in text chat, so you can move between voice and text without starting over.

With more capable models, you can talk through a half-formed idea and work out what you actually think. Claude asks follow-up questions and builds on your thinking rather than handing you an answer. Voice mode takes turns, meaning Claude listens, pauses to think, and then responds.

<!-- /bilingual:section -->

<!-- bilingual:section -->

<!-- lang:zh -->

可以试试这些：

- 演练一场重要对话，让 Claude 反馈你的沟通风格。
- 口头梳理一次客户路演，让 Claude 帮你找出逻辑漏洞。
- 为产品路线图头脑风暴想法，并让 Claude 通过竞品调研验证这些想法。
- 让 Claude 帮你评估几个不同的假设，分析你上一个视频为什么会走红。

<!-- lang:en -->

A few things to try:

- Practice for an important conversation and ask Claude for feedback on your communication style.
- Talk through a client pitch and ask Claude to find gaps in your logic.
- Brainstorm ideas for your product roadmap and ask Claude to validate them with competitive research.
- Ask Claude to help you evaluate a few different hypotheses for why your last video went viral.

<!-- /bilingual:section -->

## 从说到做 / From talking to doing

<!-- bilingual:section -->

<!-- lang:zh -->

当你决定要做什么后，就让 Claude 去执行。以下是你可以借助已连接工具完成的一些事情：

- 快迟到了？让 Claude 将 Google Calendar 上的会议推迟 30 分钟。
- 把关于客户路演的对话转化为 Canva 中的一页式文档。
- 让 Claude 总结你今天收到的邮件，并为其中最重要的几封起草回复。

Claude 在使用你已连接的工具前会先征得许可。你可以在 Claude 移动端、桌面端或网页版应用的 Settings > Connectors 中连接新工具。

<!-- lang:en -->

When you've decided what to do, ask Claude to do it. Some examples of what you can achieve in your tools:

- Running late? Ask Claude to push a meeting on your Google Calendar by 30 minutes.
- Turn a conversation about a client pitch into a one-pager in Canva.
- Ask Claude to summarize the emails you've received today and draft responses to the most critical ones.

Claude will ask for permission before using one of your connected tools. You can connect a new tool in Settings > Connectors on the Claude mobile, desktop or web apps.

<!-- /bilingual:section -->

## 用更多语言对话 / Converse in many more languages

<!-- bilingual:section -->

<!-- lang:zh -->

现在，所有套餐的语音模式都支持更多语言。你可以用自己思考时使用的语言与 Claude 对话，并在会话过程中切换。支持的语言包括：

- English（英语）
- French（法语）
- German（德语）
- Hindi（印地语）
- Indonesian（印尼语）
- Italian（意大利语）
- Japanese（日语）
- Korean（韩语）
- Portuguese (Brazilian)（巴西葡萄牙语）
- Spanish (Latin America)（拉美西班牙语）
- Spanish (Spain)（西班牙西班牙语）

<!-- lang:en -->

With many more languages now available in voice mode on every plan, you can talk with Claude in the language you think in, switching mid-conversation. Available languages include:

- English（英语）
- French（法语）
- German（德语）
- Hindi（印地语）
- Indonesian（印尼语）
- Italian（意大利语）
- Japanese（日语）
- Korean（韩语）
- Portuguese (Brazilian)（巴西葡萄牙语）
- Spanish (Latin America)（拉美西班牙语）
- Spanish (Spain)（西班牙西班牙语）

<!-- /bilingual:section -->

<!-- bilingual:section -->

<!-- lang:zh -->

直接口头告诉 Claude 从英语切换到你选择的语言，或者在语音设置的语言选择器中选择语言。

Claude 不会自动检测你的语言，因此你需要口头提出切换请求，或选择语言，才能从英语切换过去。你需要专门为语音模式设置语言，因为之前的语言设置不会自动沿用。

<!-- lang:en -->

Tell Claude out loud to switch from English to your language of choice, or select your language from the language picker in voice settings.

Claude doesn't automatically detect your language, so you'll need to ask out loud or select your language to make the switch from English. You will need to set your language specifically for voice mode, as your previous language settings will not carry over.

## 如何开始 / Getting started

<!-- bilingual:section -->

<!-- lang:zh -->

语音模式的最新更新目前以 beta 形式向所有聊天用户开放，覆盖移动端、桌面端和网页版，但在手机上的使用体验最佳。Free 套餐用户可使用 Claude Haiku、一个已连接工具以及所有可用语言。付费套餐用户可以使用更多模型和全部已连接工具。语音对话计入常规使用限额。

在 Claude 移动端应用中点击声波图标即可试用语音模式，也可以下载应用开始使用。

<!-- lang:en -->

The latest update to voice mode is available in beta to all chat users on mobile, desktop, and web, but works best from your phone. On the Free plan, you get Claude Haiku, one connected tool, and all available languages. Paid plans can access expanded models and all your connected tools. Voice conversations count toward your regular usage limits.

Try voice mode in the Claude mobile app by tapping the sound wave icon, or download the app to get started.
