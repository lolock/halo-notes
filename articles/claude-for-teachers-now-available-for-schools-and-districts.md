> **EN:** In July, we introduced [Claude for Teachers](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-for-teachers), which brought free access to premium Claude capabilities to verified U.S. K-12 educators, including teaching skills grounded in learning science and a direct connection to academic standards in all 50 states, with high-quality curricula mapped to them.

今年 7 月，Anthropic 发布了 [Claude for Teachers](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-for-teachers)，向已验证的美国 K-12 教育工作者提供免费访问 Claude 高级能力的通道。该能力包含基于学习科学构建的教学技能，并与 50 个州的学业标准对齐，有高质量课程内容进行映射。

> **EN:** Starting today, Claude for Teachers is available to schools and districts as a free Enterprise offering. School and district leaders can now bring educators and staff into one centrally managed organization under a single set of K-12 Terms, with [enterprise features](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/16559896) like single sign on, role-based access controls, and domain claiming.

自今日起，Claude for Teachers 进一步扩展为面向学校和学区的企业级免费方案。学校和学区领导可以将教职员工纳入同一个集中管理组织，统一适用一套 K-12 条款，并获得[企业级能力](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/16559896)，如单点登录、基于角色的访问控制以及域名接管。

### 使用方式 / How it works

> **EN:** A school or district administrator gets verified, accepts the K-12 terms and student data privacy agreement, and connects their email domain and single sign-on. From there:

学校或学区管理员完成验证后，需要同意 K-12 条款和学生数据隐私协议，并绑定邮箱域名与单点登录。之后流程如下：

> **EN:** 
> - **Educators and school staff get free access to Claude for Teachers.** This includes Claude capabilities for K-12 educators like teaching skills, state standards and the high-quality curricula beneath them, and connections to the K-12 tools teachers already use.
> - **Usage limits match individual Claude for Teachers accounts, at no cost.** Overage billing is turned off by default unless administrators opt in.
> - **The school or district administrators manage the accounts.** Qualified schools and districts receive our suite of [Enterprise features](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/16559896), like role-based access control and single sign-on. Admins add and remove staff, set policies, and see adoption across schools.
> - **Through domain capture,** teachers who already verified on the school or district's domain move into the centrally managed account.
> - **The school or district holds the terms and privacy agreement.** Anthropic provides school and district-level K-12 Terms and Data Processing Agreement, so one set of FERPA-aligned commitments applies across your entire organization. Claude for Teachers data is not used for model training purposes, and student information is protected by our K-12 Data Processing Agreement. You can read more about K-12 privacy in Claude for Teachers [here](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/15926041-claude-for-teachers-your-data-and-our-terms).

- 校方和学区的教师与员工可免费使用 Claude for Teachers。对应能力包括面向 K-12 的教学技能、州学标对齐能力，以及与现有教学工具生态的衔接。
- 用量限制与个人版 Claude for Teachers 保持一致且免费；超额计费默认关闭，只有管理员主动开启后才会启用。
- 学校/学区管理员管理账号与成员。符合条件的组织可通过[企业级功能](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/16559896)进行角色权限控制和 SSO 管理，按需增减成员，并可查看各校覆盖与使用情况。
- 借助“域名收拢（domain capture）”，已在组织域名下验证过的教师会自动并入该集中账号。
- 学校和学区承担统一的条款与隐私协议。Anthropic 提供 K-12 条款和数据处理协议，确保组织内统一采用 FERPA 对齐的承诺；Claude for Teachers 数据不会用于模型训练。关于 K-12 隐私条款，可在 [Claude for Teachers 说明页](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/15926041-claude-for-teachers-your-data-and-our-terms)查看。

### 开学季新增内容 / New for back-to-school

> **EN:** We’re also adding new and improved resources for educators heading back to school, including:

我们也为返校季的教师准备了更多新增与改进资源，主要包括：

> **EN:**
> - **Two new teaching skills**, co-developed with Learning Commons. *Lesson preparation*: Claude helps you plan for the key moments of an upcoming lesson, so you're prepared and confident. *Check for understanding*: Build short standards-aligned checks for understanding that surface what students are thinking, so you know what to teach next. Available for math at launch.
> - **Improvements across existing skills.** We’ve made accessibility upgrades to student-facing materials and incorporated a lesson preparation step into our lesson planning skill.
> - **Updated** [**Claude for K-12 Academy**](https://academy.claude.com/collections/k-12). **Free AI fluency content including courses, guides, and classroom workflows for K-12 teachers, coaches, and district teams.**

- 与 Learning Commons 共同推出的两个新教学技能：
  - 课堂准备：帮助你提前规划关键教学节点，让课前准备更充分更自信。
  - 理解度检测：快速生成符合标准的“理解核验”题目，帮助你看见学生思维走向，从而决定下一步讲解内容；本次上线先支持数学。
- 对现有技能做了可访问性优化，学生端材料进行了可用性增强，并在课程规划技能里补充了“备课步骤”。
- 全新更新 [Claude for K-12 Academy](https://academy.claude.com/collections/k-12)，提供免费 AI 素养课程、操作指南和课堂流程模板，面向老师、教研长和学区团队。

> **EN:** This is part of Anthropic's broader efforts in education, focused on supporting teachers and improving learning outcomes for students. Through our [Beneficial Deployments](https://www.anthropic.com/beneficial-deployments) mission, we continue to [publish teaching skills as public goods](https://github.com/anthropics/k12-teacher-skills) for the community of developers building for teachers. This fall, we are also piloting an evaluation of Claude for Teachers in the Detroit Public Schools Community District, working closely with teachers to study the impact on educator wellbeing and practice.

这是 Anthropic 教育方向的持续举措，目标是在支撑教师的同时改善学生学习成效。通过[Beneficial Deployments](https://www.anthropic.com/beneficial-deployments)使命，我们继续向开发者社区公开发布教学技能，鼓励围绕教师场景的共建共研（见 [k12-teacher-skills](https://github.com/anthropics/k12-teacher-skills)）。本秋季，Anthropic 还将在底特律公立学区做真实使用评估，与一线教师一起观察其对教师福祉和教学实践的影响。

## 开始使用 / Getting started

> **EN:** U.S. K-12 school and district leaders can verify their district and [get started](https://claude.com/solutions/teachers#get-verified) today. Qualifying organizations can sign up by June 30, 2027, for a full year of free access.

美国 K-12 学校与学区负责人可先完成组织验证，然后[立即开始使用](https://claude.com/solutions/teachers#get-verified)。符合条件的组织可在 2027 年 6 月 30 日前报名，获得一年完整免费额度。
