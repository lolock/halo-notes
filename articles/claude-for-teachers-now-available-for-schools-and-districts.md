## 面向学校和学区开放 / Available for schools and districts

<!-- bilingual:section -->

<!-- lang:zh -->

今年 7 月，Anthropic 发布了 [Claude for Teachers](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-for-teachers)，向经过验证的美国 K-12 教育工作者免费提供 Claude 的高级能力，包括基于学习科学的教学技能，以及与全美 50 个州学业标准直接衔接、并映射至这些标准的高质量课程内容。

自今日起，Claude for Teachers 也作为免费的 Enterprise 方案面向学校和学区开放。学校和学区负责人现在可以将教师与员工纳入一个集中管理的组织，统一适用一套 K-12 条款，并使用[企业级功能](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/16559896)，例如单点登录、基于角色的访问控制和域名认领。

<!-- lang:en -->

In July, we introduced [Claude for Teachers](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-for-teachers), which brought free access to premium Claude capabilities to verified U.S. K-12 educators, including teaching skills grounded in learning science and a direct connection to academic standards in all 50 states, with high-quality curricula mapped to them.

Starting today, Claude for Teachers is available to schools and districts as a free Enterprise offering. School and district leaders can now bring educators and staff into one centrally managed organization under a single set of K-12 Terms, with [enterprise features](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/16559896) like single sign on, role-based access controls, and domain claiming.

<!-- /bilingual:section -->

## 使用方式 / How it works

<!-- bilingual:section -->

<!-- lang:zh -->

学校或学区管理员完成验证、接受 K-12 条款和学生数据隐私协议，并关联邮箱域名与单点登录后，流程如下：

- **教师和学校员工可免费使用 Claude for Teachers。** 其中包括面向 K-12 教育工作者的 Claude 能力，例如教学技能、州级标准及其对应的高质量课程内容，以及与教师已经在使用的 K-12 工具的连接。
- **用量限制与个人 Claude for Teachers 账号一致，且无需付费。** 除非管理员主动选择启用，否则默认关闭超额计费。
- **学校或学区管理员负责管理账号。** 符合条件的学校和学区可使用[企业级功能](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/16559896)，例如基于角色的访问控制和单点登录。管理员可以添加和移除员工、设置政策，并查看各校的采用情况。
- **通过域名收拢，** 已在学校或学区域名下完成验证的教师会转入集中管理的账号。
- **学校或学区承担条款与隐私协议。** Anthropic 提供学校和学区级别的 K-12 条款与数据处理协议，因此整个组织统一适用一套符合 FERPA 要求的承诺。Claude for Teachers 数据不会用于模型训练，学生信息则受到 K-12 数据处理协议的保护。关于 Claude for Teachers 中的 K-12 隐私，详见[此处](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/15926041-claude-for-teachers-your-data-and-our-terms)。

<!-- lang:en -->

A school or district administrator gets verified, accepts the K-12 terms and student data privacy agreement, and connects their email domain and single sign-on. From there:

- **Educators and school staff get free access to Claude for Teachers.** This includes Claude capabilities for K-12 educators like teaching skills, state standards and the high-quality curricula beneath them, and connections to the K-12 tools teachers already use.
- **Usage limits match individual Claude for Teachers accounts, at no cost.** Overage billing is turned off by default unless administrators opt in.
- **The school or district administrators manage the accounts.** Qualified schools and districts receive our suite of [Enterprise features](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/16559896), like role-based access control and single sign-on. Admins add and remove staff, set policies, and see adoption across schools.
- **Through domain capture,** teachers who already verified on the school or district's domain move into the centrally managed account.
- **The school or district holds the terms and privacy agreement.** Anthropic provides school and district-level K-12 Terms and Data Processing Agreement, so one set of FERPA-aligned commitments applies across your entire organization. Claude for Teachers data is not used for model training purposes, and student information is protected by our K-12 Data Processing Agreement. You can read more about K-12 privacy in Claude for Teachers [here](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/15926041-claude-for-teachers-your-data-and-our-terms).

## 开学季新增内容 / New for back-to-school

<!-- bilingual:section -->

<!-- lang:zh -->

我们也为即将返校的教育工作者新增并改进了一系列资源，包括：

- **与 Learning Commons 共同开发的两项新教学技能。** *课堂准备*：Claude 帮助你规划即将开展的课程中的关键时刻，让你准备更充分、更有信心。*理解度检测*：构建简短且符合标准的理解度检测，呈现学生正在思考的内容，帮助你确定下一步该教什么。上线时先支持数学。
- **对现有技能的改进。** 我们提升了面向学生的材料的可访问性，并在课程规划技能中加入了备课步骤。
- **更新后的** [**Claude for K-12 Academy**](https://academy.claude.com/collections/k-12)。为 K-12 教师、教练和学区团队提供免费的 AI 素养内容，包括课程、指南和课堂工作流程。

<!-- lang:en -->

We’re also adding new and improved resources for educators heading back to school, including:

- **Two new teaching skills**, co-developed with Learning Commons. *Lesson preparation*: Claude helps you plan for the key moments of an upcoming lesson, so you're prepared and confident. *Check for understanding*: Build short standards-aligned checks for understanding that surface what students are thinking, so you know what to teach next. Available for math at launch.
- **Improvements across existing skills.** We’ve made accessibility upgrades to student-facing materials and incorporated a lesson preparation step into our lesson planning skill.
- **Updated** [**Claude for K-12 Academy**](https://academy.claude.com/collections/k-12). **Free AI fluency content including courses, guides, and classroom workflows for K-12 teachers, coaches, and district teams.**

## 教育领域的持续行动 / Broader efforts in education

<!-- bilingual:section -->

<!-- lang:zh -->

这是 Anthropic 在教育领域更广泛行动的一部分，重点在于支持教师、改善学生的学习成效。通过[Beneficial Deployments](https://www.anthropic.com/beneficial-deployments)使命，我们继续向为教师开发产品的开发者社区[公开发布教学技能，作为公共资源](https://github.com/anthropics/k12-teacher-skills)。今年秋季，我们还将在底特律公立学校学区开展 Claude for Teachers 评估试点，与教师密切合作，研究其对教师福祉和教学实践的影响。

<!-- lang:en -->

This is part of Anthropic's broader efforts in education, focused on supporting teachers and improving learning outcomes for students. Through our [Beneficial Deployments](https://www.anthropic.com/beneficial-deployments) mission, we continue to [publish teaching skills as public goods](https://github.com/anthropics/k12-teacher-skills) for the community of developers building for teachers. This fall, we are also piloting an evaluation of Claude for Teachers in the Detroit Public Schools Community District, working closely with teachers to study the impact on educator wellbeing and practice.

## 开始使用 / Getting started

<!-- bilingual:section -->

<!-- lang:zh -->

美国 K-12 学校和学区负责人今天即可完成学区验证并[开始使用](https://claude.com/solutions/teachers#get-verified)。符合条件的组织可在 2027 年 6 月 30 日前注册，获得整整一年的免费使用权限。

<!-- lang:en -->

U.S. K-12 school and district leaders can verify their district and [get started](https://claude.com/solutions/teachers#get-verified) today. Qualifying organizations can sign up by June 30, 2027, for a full year of free access.
