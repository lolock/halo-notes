Claude Cowork 正在向移动端和网页端推出，意味着你的会话和文件将跟随你到达任何设备。Beta 访问将在未来几周内逐步开放，从 Max 用户开始，后续覆盖更多订阅方案。

> EN: Claude Cowork is rolling out to mobile and web so your sessions and files go where you go, on any device. Beta access is rolling out over the next several weeks starting with Max users, with more plans to follow.

## 工作之外的工作 / The work around the work

Cowork 是你将任务交给 Claude 的地方，它会在你的文件、日历、电子邮件、消息应用、网页以及你连接的其他工具之间工作，直到任务完成。

> EN: Cowork is where you hand Claude a task, and it works across your files, calendar, email, messaging app, the web, and the other tools you connect until the job is done.

AI agent 的声誉始于写代码。但当我们审视人们使用 Claude Cowork 的方式时，超过 90% 的使用场景并非软件开发。绝大多数是日常知识工作，最大的两个类别是业务运营和内容创作：核对季度开支并撰写差异说明、将一文件夹合同转化为带有风险标记的续约追踪表、从通话记录和 pipeline 数据搭建明天的客户演示文稿。这两类加在一起大约占全部使用量的一半。这就是"工作之外的工作"：很少出现在任何人的职位描述中，却占据了每个人每周的大量时间。

> EN: AI agents made their name writing code. But when we looked at how people use Claude Cowork, more than 90% of it wasn't software development. Most of it was everyday knowledge work, and the largest categories were business operations and content creation: reconciling the quarter's spend and drafting the variance memo, turning a folder of contracts into a renewals tracker with the risks flagged, building tomorrow's client deck from call transcripts and pipeline data. Together, that's roughly half of all usage. It's the work around the work: rarely in anyone's job description, but a large share of everyone's week.

## 今天的变化 / What changes today

每个人都在向 AI 寻求答案。把工作交给它则是另一回事，而人们不断给 Claude 更大型的任务。这样的工作不可能一次完成——它在夜晚、会议间隙、通勤途中不断累积。在此之前，Cowork 只存在于你的笔记本上，一旦离开，工作就停止了。现在不再如此。三件事发生了变化：

> EN: Everyone asks AI for answers. Handing it the work is different, and people keep giving Claude bigger jobs. Work like that doesn't fit in one sitting. It accumulates overnight, between meetings, on the train. Until today, Cowork lived on your laptop, so the work stopped when you stepped away. Now it doesn't. Three things change:

**你的工作跟随你。** 在桌面上启动任务，在手机上检查进度，在任何地方获取最终成品。

> EN: **Your work follows you.** Start a task at your desk, check on it from your phone, and pick up the finished output anywhere.

**工作在后台持续进行。** 合上笔记本去开会吧，Claude 继续运转。计划任务现在无需设备在线即可运行。把周一的客户准备工作安排在早上 6 点：Claude 会梳理邮件线程、通话记录和近期新闻，构建简报文档，并将后续邮件起草但尚未发送。喝杯咖啡时再过目。

> EN: **Work continues in the background.** Close the laptop and head to your meeting; Claude keeps going. Scheduled tasks now run with no device online. Set Monday's client prep for 6 am: Claude works through the email threads, transcripts, and recent news, builds the briefing doc, and leaves the follow-up email drafted but unsent. Review it over coffee.

**决策仍回到你手中。** 当 Claude 遇到只有你能拍的板时，它会询问，问题会到达你的手机上。你可以在会议中途调整草稿方向，Claude 继续沿着正确的路径前进。任何制品都未经你审核批准不得发布。

> EN: **The decisions still come to you.** When Claude reaches a call only you can make, it asks, and the question reaches your phone. You can redirect a draft mid-meeting and Claude keeps going, on the right path. Nothing ships until you've reviewed and approved it.

> "在旅行中我搭了一个客户仪表盘。我在笔记本上开始，在等行李时从手机上继续。会话就这样无缝延续。" — Armmand Hosseini，Ramp 客户成功部门

> EN: "I built a dashboard to track my clients while traveling. I started on my laptop and picked the session up on my phone while waiting for my bag to come out. It just held the thread." — Armmand Hosseini, Customer Success, Ramp

桌面仍然是深度工作的场所，也是完整的 Cowork 体验所在——Claude 在这里还可以使用你的本地文件和浏览器。无法安装桌面应用的用户现在也可以使用 Cowork 了。

> EN: Desktop remains the place for deep work, and it's the full Cowork experience, where Claude can also use your local files and browser. Users who couldn't install a desktop app can now use Cowork too.

你还会注意到另外两个变化：在网页端和桌面端，聊天与 Cowork 现在共享同一个入口，你的项目和 artifacts 在两者之间共同存在。委托任务和提问一样简单了。

> EN: Two more changes you'll notice: on web and desktop, chat and Cowork now share one home, and your projects and artifacts live together across both. Delegating is now as easy as asking.

## 开始使用 / Getting started

在网页端，从 claude.ai 的主页启动 Cowork 会话。在移动端，从 Claude 的 iOS 和 Android 应用侧边栏打开 Cowork。完整的体验请下载 Claude 桌面应用。各平台的功能差异请参阅帮助中心。

> EN: On the web, start a Cowork session from the home screen at claude.ai. On mobile, open Cowork from the sidebar of the Claude app for iOS and Android. For the full experience, download the Claude desktop app. For what's available on each surface, see the Help Center.

从你手头已有的工作开始：把文件夹、邮件线程或半成品幻灯片指向 Claude，描述"做完"的样子。为庆祝此次发布，我们将双倍 Cowork 用量上限延长至 8 月 5 日，所以有足够空间尝试更大的任务。

> EN: Start with something already on your plate: point Claude at the folder, the thread, or the half-finished deck, and describe what done looks like. To mark the launch, we're extending doubled Cowork usage limits through August 5, so there's plenty of room to try bigger tasks.
