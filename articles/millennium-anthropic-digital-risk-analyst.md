> **EN:** Anthropic is working with Millennium, one of the world's largest alternative investment management firms, to co-develop a digital risk analyst: an AI teammate to work alongside and under the supervision of the firm's risk managers to surface new risk insights and form opinions on risk exposure across asset classes.

Anthropic 正在与 Millennium 合作——这家公司是全球规模最大的另类投资管理公司之一——共同开发一款「数字风险分析师」（digital risk analyst）：一个 AI 队友，在公司的风险经理的监督下并肩工作，帮助发现新的风险洞察，并对跨资产类别的风险敞口形成判断。

> **EN:** Claude and [Claude Code](https://claude.com/product/claude-code) are already used broadly across Millennium today - from trading desks and engineering to core business functions. Millennium's digital risk analyst extends their use of Claude by helping risk managers expedite and enrich analysis of risk positions.

如今，Claude 和 [Claude Code](https://claude.com/product/claude-code) 已被 Millennium 广泛应用——从交易台、工程团队到核心业务部门。Millennium 的数字风险分析师进一步拓展了他们对 Claude 的使用，帮助风险经理加快并深化对风险头寸的分析。

## 用 Claude 评估金融风险 / Evaluating financial risk with Claude

> **EN:** Investment firms have spent decades using technology to improve risk measurement, moving from traditional assessments to more complex real-time scenario analysis. Millennium's digital risk analyst is designed to tackle critical workflows, powered by Millennium's proprietary data and Claude's frontier intelligence.

投资公司花费了数十年时间，用技术改进风险度量，从传统评估逐步走向更复杂的实时情景分析。Millennium 的数字风险分析师正是为处理这些关键工作流而设计，由 Millennium 的专有数据与 Claude 的前沿智能共同驱动。

> **EN:** The digital risk analyst retains and recalls information over time, applying new reasoning capabilities to help explain daily risk changes. These findings are then validated and enriched by Millennium's human risk managers.

数字风险分析师能够随时间保留并回忆信息，运用新的推理能力帮助解释每日的风险变化。这些发现随后会由 Millennium 的人类风险经理进行验证和补充。

> **EN:** "At Millennium, we believe AI can help set a new bar for what our people can achieve," said Vlad Torgovnik, Chief Information Officer, at Millennium. "Our work with Anthropic is a great example of this and shows how AI is driving innovation in core parts of our business while keeping human judgment at the center of decision making. We are excited to continue our work with an industry leader finding new ways AI can add value to our business."

「在 Millennium，我们相信 AI 能够帮助我们的员工把成就提升到新的高度，」Millennium 首席信息官 Vlad Torgovnik 表示。「我们与 Anthropic 的合作就是一个很好的例子，它展示了 AI 如何在我们业务的核心环节推动创新，同时让人类判断始终处于决策的中心。我们很高兴能与行业领导者继续合作，探索 AI 为我们的业务创造价值的新方式。」

## Millennium 员工如何使用 Claude / How Millennium employees use Claude

> **EN:** Millennium was an early adopter of Claude and Claude Code. Employees use Claude Code to write software, build products, and improve workflows, including across many of Millennium's 340+ investment teams.

Millennium 是 Claude 和 Claude Code 的早期采用者。员工使用 Claude Code 编写软件、构建产品并改进工作流，应用范围覆盖 Millennium 的 340 多个投资团队。

> **EN:** The digital risk analyst provides secure, auditable analysis by logging its reasoning, testing its actions in sandboxed environments, and requiring human experts to evaluate and approve its decisions. Millennium's risk experts are building it with Anthropic's research and applied AI teams working alongside them in Millennium's AI lab.

数字风险分析师提供安全、可审计的分析：它会记录自己的推理过程，在沙盒环境中测试自己的操作，并要求人类专家评估和批准其决策。Millennium 的风险专家正在与 Anthropic 的研究与应用 AI 团队合作构建它，Anthropic 的团队就在 Millennium 的 AI 实验室中与他们并肩工作。

> **EN:** Millennium is using its internal AI lab to continue pushing the frontier, pressure testing Anthropic's latest Claude models against ambitious use cases, and putting Claude to work innovating in a fast-paced environment.

Millennium 正在利用其内部 AI 实验室持续探索前沿，用雄心勃勃的用例对 Anthropic 最新的 Claude 模型进行压力测试，并让 Claude 在快节奏的环境中投入创新工作。

> **EN:** "Financial services requires AI that people can trust in complex, demanding environments," said Belinda Neal, Managing Director, Financial Services, at Anthropic. "Millennium has been an early adopter of Claude and Claude Code, and our work with its risk teams will show how AI can inform the analysis that risk managers at Millennium make every day."

「金融服务需要的是在复杂、严苛环境中值得信赖的 AI，」Anthropic 金融服务董事总经理 Belinda Neal 表示。「Millennium 一直是 Claude 和 Claude Code 的早期采用者，我们与其风险团队的合作将展示 AI 如何为 Millennium 风险经理每天的日常分析提供支持。」

> **EN:** "Claude can reason through risk positions, explain daily changes, and carry what it learned into the next question," said Peter Nolan, Head of Asset and Wealth Management, at Anthropic. "Millennium's risk managers will use frontier intelligence to deliver automated recommendations with the goal of saving valuable time."

「Claude 能够对风险头寸进行推理、解释每日变化，并把学到的内容带入下一个问题，」Anthropic 资产与财富管理负责人 Peter Nolan 表示。「Millennium 的风险经理将利用前沿智能提供自动化建议，以节省宝贵的时间。」
