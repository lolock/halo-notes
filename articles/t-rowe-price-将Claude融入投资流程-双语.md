# T. Rowe Price 在其投资流程中引入更多 Claude / T. Rowe Price brings more of Claude to its investment process

> EN: T. Rowe Price, a global investment management firm, and Anthropic today announced the expansion of Claude across the firm’s investment organization. Portfolio managers and analysts at the global investment management firm are working with Claude and Claude Cowork on their research, and its developers are building investment tools with Claude Code.
>
> ZH: 全球投资管理公司 T. Rowe Price 与 Anthropic 今日宣布，进一步将 Claude 在公司投资组织中全面扩展。公司的投资组合经理和分析师已开始在研究工作中与 Claude 与 Claude Cowork 协作，开发团队也在使用 Claude Code 构建投资相关工具。

> EN: T. Rowe Price is an active manager, so its investment decisions rest on its own fundamental research. Analysts and portfolio managers read and evaluate large volumes of information, then apply their judgment to reach a view. The firm's goal with using Claude is to give them more capacity for this type of work.
>
> ZH: T. Rowe Price 属于主动管理公司，因此投资决策基于自身的基本面研究。分析师和投资组合经理需要消化海量信息，并结合主观判断形成投资观点。公司引入 Claude 的目标，是为这类工作增加产能。

## T. Rowe Price 使用 Claude 的方式 / How T. Rowe Price uses Claude

> EN: The firm is using Claude for three areas of the investment process:
>
> ZH: 公司目前把 Claude 应用于投资流程中的三个领域：

- **Research / 研究**

  > EN: Research. Investment professionals ask Cowork to synthesize complex information, which leaves more time for analysis and debate.
  >
  > ZH: 研究。投资从业者让 Cowork 负责梳理和综合复杂信息，从而释放出更多时间用于深入分析与讨论。

- **Multi-step processes / 多步骤流程**

  > EN: Multi-step processes. Teams work with Cowork on knowledge-intensive tasks where consistency and oversight matter.
  >
  > ZH: 多步骤流程。团队在 Cowork 中协作处理知识密集型任务，在此类任务中，流程一致性与监督机制尤为关键。

- **Tool building / 工具构建**

  > EN: Tool building. Developers work with Claude Code to build and improve build and improve tools that support investment and operating teams.
  >
  > ZH: 工具构建。开发者与 Claude Code 配合，持续建设并优化支持投资与运营团队的工具能力。

> EN: All of this runs under the AI leadership model T. Rowe Price announced in August, which places AI leaders inside Investments and Global Distribution and charges T. Rowe Price Labs, the firm’s enterprise AI and data innovation hub, with evaluating and scaling AI across the firm. Each application is owned by the business, governed by the firm's standards for responsible use, and built so that human judgment and accountability stay at the center of client outcomes.
>
> ZH: 这套做法延续了 T. Rowe Price 8 月发布的 AI 治理模式：AI 负责人嵌入投资与全球分销两条主线，T. Rowe Price Labs（公司企业级 AI 与数据创新中心）负责评估并在公司内推动 AI 落地。每个应用由对应业务线负责，遵循负责任 AI 的公司标准开发，确保人类判断与问责始终位于客户结果的核心。

> EN: "Most enterprise AI deployments start with the back office. T. Rowe Price started with the people who pick the securities," said Peter Nolan, Head of Asset and Wealth Management at Anthropic.
>
> ZH: Anthropic 资产与财富管理负责人 Peter Nolan 表示："大多数企业级 AI 落地先从后台开始；而 T. Rowe Price 的起点是那些选股的人。"

> EN: Eric Veiel, President, Co-head of Global Investments, and Chief Investment Officer at T. Rowe Price, described Claude as an extension of a research process that has served the firm's clients for nearly 90 years. "Claude lets our investment professionals cover more ground and go deeper on what matters," he said.
>
> ZH: T. Rowe Price 全球投资联合负责人兼首席投资官 Eric Veiel 将 Claude 形容为延续公司近 90 年服务客户研究体系的延展工具。他表示："Claude 让我们的投资专业人员能够覆盖更多议题，并在关键问题上做更深入的判断。"

## 下一步 / What's next

> EN: This work fits into T. Rowe Price’s broader AI efforts, which include training, talent development, and responsible-use guidance to help associates build confidence using AI while keeping human judgment and client focus front and center.The firm's stated ambition is to lead the investment management industry in how AI is applied to investing, to running the firm, and to serving clients.
>
> ZH: 这些举措与 T. Rowe Price 的 broader AI 战略一体联动：公司同步推进 AI 培训、人才培养与合规指引，目标是提升同事对 AI 的使用信心，同时让人类判断和客户导向始终居中。公司层面的长期目标是引领资产管理行业，定义 AI 在投资、运营管理与客户服务中的正确应用方式。
