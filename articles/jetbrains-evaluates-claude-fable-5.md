Vladislav Tankov, CTO at JetBrains, spoke with Anthropic about how his team evaluates new models, decides when to use Claude Fable 5, and thinks about data retention and safeguards when working with frontier models.

JetBrains 打造了全球开发者使用的各类工具，从 IntelliJ IDEA、PyCharm 到 Kotlin 编程语言，服务超过 1250 万活跃用户，以及《财富》全球 100 强中的 88 家企业。JetBrains 首席技术官 Vladislav Tankov 与 Anthropic 聊了聊他的团队如何评估新模型、何时决定使用 Claude Fable 5，以及在使用前沿模型时如何看待数据留存与安全防护。

## 2026 年，前沿 AI 给 JetBrains 带来了哪些变化？ / How has frontier AI changed for JetBrains in 2026?

> **EN:** I've been with JetBrains for 10 years, and we were among the very first customers of LLM providers. Over the last year, we moved from having AI skeptics among our customers and inside the company to seeing that AI is here to stay. It's a big and foundational change in the technology industry. Literally every skeptic in the company has changed.

我在 JetBrains 已经工作了 10 年，我们是最早一批 LLM 提供商的客户。过去一年，我们经历了一场转变：从客户和公司内部都存在 AI 怀疑论者，到所有人都认识到 AI 已经势不可挡。这是科技行业一个巨大而根本性的变化。公司里每一个怀疑者都改变了看法。

## 你们如何评估新模型，并决定何时使用它们？ / How do you evaluate new models and decide when to use them?

> **EN:** We're a coding company, so we have a big evaluation pipeline: large eval sets on private repositories, including our monorepo. We take a close look at whether a model lives up to its benchmark scores on real work—some models are tuned to score well on public benchmarks but fall down on actual tasks. With a private repository, that's a lot easier to check. We also keep leaderboards for best quality, best cost per task, and fastest model. While Claude Fable 5 is more expensive per token, its cost per task is lower in some cases, particularly for more complicated, long-running work.

我们是一家做编码工具的公司，所以拥有一条庞大的评估流水线：在私有代码库（包括我们的单体仓库 monorepo）上运行大规模的评估集。我们会仔细考察模型在真实工作中是否对得起它的基准分数——有些模型专门调优到在公开基准上拿高分，但一遇到实际任务就露馅。有了私有仓库，这一点就很容易检验。我们还维护着几份排行榜：最佳质量、单任务成本最低、速度最快。虽然 Claude Fable 5 每 token 更贵，但在某些情况下它的单任务成本反而更低，尤其是更复杂、耗时更长的任务。

## 在你们的评估中，Claude Fable 5 相比之前的模型表现如何？ / How did Claude Fable 5 score on your evals relative to previous models?

> **EN:** Claude Fable 5 is both more accurate and more efficient than prior models. It posted the best Python pass rate in our suite at 44.3%, against 28.2% for Opus 4.8, a 16-point jump. In a head-to-head comparison, Claude Fable 5 solved 18 Python tasks that Opus 4.8 missed and lost only 2. Its answers are also more trustworthy: when its code ran, it passed our tests far more often than either Opus model. That matters because code that runs but produces wrong answers is the most expensive kind of failure to catch.

Claude Fable 5 相比之前的模型既更准确，也更高效。它在我们的测试套件中取得了最高的 Python 通过率 44.3%，而 Opus 4.8 是 28.2%，提升了 16 个百分点。在正面交锋中，Claude Fable 5 解决了 18 个 Opus 4.8 没做出来的 Python 任务，只输了 2 个。它的回答也更可信：当它生成的代码真正运行时，通过我们测试的概率远高于两个 Opus 模型。这一点很重要，因为“能运行但答案错误”的代码是最难被发现、代价也最高的一种失败。

> **EN:** The efficiency story is just as interesting. Claude Fable 5 needed about 22% fewer steps than Opus 4.8 to reach a solution, so it gets to working code with less trial and error. It also spends its effort in the right places. On Java tasks, Opus 4.8 repeatedly tried to pull in outside resources that almost never help in our environment, while Claude Fable 5 skipped that entirely and worked with the code in front of it. It shows better engineering habits more generally.

效率方面的故事同样有趣。Claude Fable 5 达到解决方案所需的步骤比 Opus 4.8 少约 22%，也就是说它能用更少的试错走到可运行的代码。它也会把力气花在正确的地方：在 Java 任务上，Opus 4.8 反复尝试引入外部资源，而这些资源在我们的环境中几乎从来帮不上忙；Claude Fable 5 则完全跳过了这一步，直接处理手头的代码。这体现出它整体上更好的工程习惯。

## 你们在什么情况下会选用 Claude Fable 5 而不是其他模型？ / When do you use Claude Fable 5 over other models?

> **EN:** Opus is seen as a workhorse: you can be very sure it will do the work. You go to Claude Fable 5 when you really need good reasoning, when you almost need a partner, and you're not sure yourself how to do the thing. For example, one of our tech leads decided to implement a rich text editor component we had attempted a few times over the years, and Claude Fable 5 almost one-shotted it.

Opus 被视为“老黄牛”：你可以很确定它会把活干完。而当你真正需要出色的推理、几乎需要一个搭档、连自己都不确定该怎么做时，就会去找 Claude Fable 5。比如，我们一位技术负责人决定实现一个富文本编辑器组件——这个组件我们多年来尝试过好几次——Claude Fable 5 几乎一次就搞定了。

> **EN:** Another popular Claude Fable 5 use case is long-running agentic-coding experimentation. We provide an agent running Claude Fable 5 with specifications (in the form of text and images) and make it implement sophisticated IDE-like apps. The interesting thing here is that specifications can also be generated by the agent, based on the existing app. Joining these two components allows us to rewrite the app from one runtime, framework, or language to another in a nearly black-box setup.

另一个流行的 Claude Fable 5 用例是长时间运行的智能体编码实验。我们给运行着 Claude Fable 5 的智能体提供规格说明（以文本和图片的形式），让它实现复杂的类 IDE 应用。有意思的是，这些规格说明也可以由智能体基于现有应用自行生成。把这两部分结合起来，我们就能在近乎黑盒的设定下，把应用从一个运行时、框架或语言重写到另一个。

## 面对如今的前沿模型，你们如何看待安全与数据留存？ / How are you thinking about safety and data retention with today's frontier models?

> **EN:** We're not a company trying to create the safest model ourselves. We expect that the red teaming and everything else done on Anthropic's side is enough to believe the model is safe. Then we take a systematic approach to deployment, where we can guarantee safety: creating the infrastructure and the safety net around the model and the harness, rather than tweaking the model itself.

我们不是一家要自己打造最安全模型的公司。我们相信 Anthropic 方面所做的红队测试和其他一切工作，足以让我们相信模型是安全的。然后我们以系统化的方式对待部署——在部署层面我们可以保证安全：围绕模型及其运行框架（harness）搭建基础设施和安全网，而不是去微调模型本身。

> **EN:** Security is also one of our biggest Claude Fable 5 uses. We run white-box testing against our own products to find vulnerabilities, and our security team is preparing for the fact that not only are we running the model—people outside the company will be running Claude Fable 5, or similar-class models, to probe for vulnerabilities across all of our products. Since we serve large enterprises in regulated industries, it's important for us to be prepared. Claude Fable 5 supports our work rather than blocking.

安全也是我们使用 Claude Fable 5 最大的场景之一。我们对自家产品进行白盒测试以发现漏洞，同时我们的安全团队正在为这样一个事实做准备：运行这个模型的不只是我们——公司外部的人也会用 Claude Fable 5 或同类模型，来扫描我们所有产品的漏洞。由于我们服务的是受监管行业的大型企业，做好准备对我们非常重要。Claude Fable 5 是助力我们的工作，而不是阻碍。

> **EN:** So it's a tight balance: the less aggressive the classifier is on your side, the more vulnerabilities someone will find in our products—including ones nobody knew about.

所以这是一个微妙的平衡：你们那边的分类器越不激进，就越会有人在我们的产品里发现漏洞——包括那些之前无人知晓的漏洞。

> **EN:** And it's no secret: we'd prefer zero data retention. But I don't see any other way for you to understand what was asked and where a classifier may have worked incorrectly. As long as reviews are only to investigate the most serious cases flagged, I'm okay with it. I think it's a fair tradeoff for access to frontier intelligence that allows my team to do their best work.

而且这算不上秘密：我们更希望零数据留存。但我想不出还有别的办法，能让你们理解用户提出了什么、分类器又可能在哪里出了错。只要这些审查仅用于调查被标记的最严重案例，我就可以接受。我认为，为了获得让我的团队发挥最佳水平的前沿智能，这是一个公平的交换。

## JetBrains 的 AI 路线图下一步是什么？ / What's next on JetBrains's AI roadmap?

> **EN:** We expect the underlying models built by the LLM providers to keep getting more capable. What matters now is a kind of cockpit for software development: a space in which agents and people collaborate, and where people can manage the development process.

我们预计 LLM 提供商构建的底层模型会变得越来越强大。现在重要的是为软件开发打造一个“驾驶舱”：一个智能体与人协作、人可以管理开发过程的空间。

> **EN:** For JetBrains, it’s a big transformation. We see an opportunity to build the next generation of products across the agentic software development lifecycle that powers that cockpit. Developers will get more and better code shipped with agents, non-technical roles will have a larger role in software creation, and organisations will get the governance and clarity on the return on investment they need.

对 JetBrains 来说，这是一次巨大的转型。我们看到了一个机会：在支撑这个驾驶舱的智能体软件开发全生命周期中，打造下一代产品。开发者将借助智能体交付更多、更好的代码；非技术角色将在软件开发中扮演更重要的角色；组织将获得所需的治理能力，以及对投资回报的清晰认知。
