> EN: More than 250,000 companies, from small and midsize businesses to Fortune 500 organizations, use monday.com to manage their work. When the company launched more than a decade ago, its core product was a visual interface that helped teams automate workflows and manage projects. Today, it has rearchitected its product from the ground up around a human-agent collaboration model where AI is woven into work at every level. With Claude at the core, monday’s new platform handles the technical complexity so customers can work at the AI frontier inside workflows they already know.
>
> ZH: 从中小企业到财富 500 强，超过 25 万家公司使用 monday.com 管理工作。monday.com 在十多年前成立时，核心产品是帮助团队自动化流程和管理项目的可视化界面；如今其平台已从底层重构为以人机协作模型为核心的架构，AI 已嵌入到各级工作中。以 Claude 为核心的重构体系处理复杂的技术细节，使客户能够在熟悉的工作流里直接进入 AI 前沿应用。

> EN: "The shift to an agent-first product was one of the most significant decisions we've made as a company," said Daniel Lereya, chief product and technology officer at monday.com. "It meant fundamentally reimagining what the platform should do, not just adding AI to existing workflows. Our vision is for monday to be the place where people and AI agents work together seamlessly and Anthropic and Claude have been trusted partners in helping us bring that vision to life."
>
> ZH: monday.com 首席产品与技术官 Daniel Lereya 表示：“转向以智能体优先的产品是公司最重要的决策之一，这意味着从根本上重新定义平台应做什么，而不仅仅是在既有流程上“外挂”AI。monday 的愿景是让平台成为人类与 AI 智能体无缝协作的空间，而 Anthropic 与 Claude 一直是实现这一愿景的可靠合作伙伴。”

## 突破“AI 粉饰”瓶颈 / Hitting the “AI dust” ceiling

> EN: monday’s rebuild unfolded in three phases. In the first phase, as frontier LLM technology matured and customer excitement grew, monday teams worked on embedding AI capabilities into its original platform. The effort culminated in May 2025 with an internal “AI month,” with four weeks dedicated to shipping AI features or products across the company.
>
> ZH: monday.com 的重构经历了三个阶段。第一阶段中，随着前沿大模型成熟与客户需求增长，团队开始在原有平台中逐步嵌入 AI 能力。到 2025 年 5 月，公司内部举行了为期四周的“AI month”，集中交付 AI 特性和产品。

> EN: Adoption was strong and generated excitement, but soon the team hit a ceiling. “We were building ‘AI dust’, sprinkling automations onto existing workflows without embedding them within or changing the product’s fundamental value proposition,” says Orly Stern Izhaki, VP of Product, AI Works Platform at monday.com. “Our features helped users summarize text and categorize information, but they weren’t creating sustained usage patterns.”
>
> ZH: 尽管采纳率和讨论热度都很高，但团队很快触到了瓶颈。Orly Stern Izhaki（monday.com AI Works Platform 产品副总裁）指出，团队曾“只在现有流程上撒了些 AI 灰尘”，即在不改变产品核心价值主张的情况下往流程里附加自动化。功能虽然能支持文本摘要、信息分类，但并未形成长期稳定的使用曲线。

> EN: The company needed to shift focus from adding AI to product features to building it natively into the platform. "Adopting AI features is not the same as becoming an AI company," Izhaki says. "Once we understood that, everything changed."
>
> ZH: 公司意识到必须把重点从“给现有特性加 AI”转向“在平台原生层面内建 AI”。Izhaki 说：采用 AI 功能并不等于“成为 AI 公司”。一旦认清这一点，平台方向就彻底改变了。

> EN: That’s how Izhaki’s team set out to reimagine monday completely: from a work management tool to a place where people and agents get work done together. While the mandate to transform the product came from the top, it was up to each team and each employee to translate that north star into concrete product choices and build their own agents.
>
> ZH: 因此 Izhaki 团队开始彻底重塑 monday 的定位：从传统工作管理工具，变成让人和智能体共同完成任务的协作平台。虽然“重建平台”的大方向由高层定下，但如何把方向落地为具体产品决策与代理设计，则由各团队和员工共同完成。

> EN: After months of intense work, the company announced the most significant change in its history, rebuilding its entire product experience around humans and agents working together, using already built-in context, workflows, boards, permissions, and governance. Since launching in May 2026, monday’s customers have had more than 5 million interactions with agents on its platform.
>
> ZH: 在数月高强度推进后，公司宣布史上最重大变更：重建完整产品体验，让人和智能体协同工作，依托既有上下文、流程、看板、权限和治理能力。自 2026 年 5 月上线以来，客户在平台上的智能体交互已超过 500 万次。

## 智能体成为同事 / Agents as teammates

> EN: Along with access permissions and restrictions, each monday agent is given a name and an avatar, and colleagues can assign agents work through triggers and mentions in the monday platform.
>
> ZH: 每个 monday 智能体都拥有名称与头像，并在访问权限与限制框架内被分配任务，团队成员可在平台内通过触发器和提及（mention）给智能体派发工作。

> EN: This design was intentional, addressing a pattern monday noticed across its customer base: many enterprises want to put AI to work, but often stall at an AI chat that runs parallel to where they actually do the work. Embedding agents directly into workflows and enabling people to interact with them like they would with their colleagues turned agentic AI from an abstract concept to a concrete, actionable one.
>
> ZH: 这一设计并非偶然，而是回应了客户共同遇到的现象：企业想用 AI，但常停留在与实际工作流程平行的聊天窗口。通过把智能体直接放进实际流程，并让员工像协作同事一样与其互动，agentic AI 从抽象概念变成了可执行的工作方式。

> EN: The jobs monday has mapped for agents range from IT ticket triage and knowledge-base upkeep to candidate sourcing and interview scheduling, competitive-intelligence briefings for sales and marketing, and chief-of-staff work like meeting prep and converting decisions into tracked tasks.
>
> ZH: monday 为智能体定义的任务覆盖范围很广：从 IT 工单分流、知识库维护，到候选人筛选与面试安排、销售和市场竞争情报简报，再到像会议准备、将决议转化为可跟踪任务等准内勤工作。

- EN: Intake & Triage Agent — classify tickets, auto-resolve common requests, escalate with full context
- ZH: 进件与分流智能体：分类工单、自动处理常见请求，并携带完整上下文升级处理
- EN: Knowledge Agent — detect knowledge gaps, draft new KB articles
- ZH: 知识智能体：识别知识缺口、起草新知识库条目
- EN: Incident Agent — detect incidents, open war rooms, trigger post-mortems
- ZH: 事故智能体：发现事件、建立 War Room、触发复盘流程
- EN: Resume Screener — score applications, surface top candidates, send rejections
- ZH: 简历筛选智能体：评分候选人、展示优秀候选、发送拒绝通知
- EN: Interview Scheduler — handle all scheduling and confirmations
- ZH: 面试安排智能体：处理时间安排与确认流程
- EN: Hiring Coordinator — keep all stakeholders updated throughout the process, so there is always a human in the loop
- ZH: 招聘协调智能体：在全过程中向各方同步状态，确保始终有人类在关键环节把关
- EN: Feedback Manager — collect structured interviewer feedback automatically
- ZH: 反馈管理智能体：自动收集结构化面试反馈
- EN: Competitive Intelligence Agent — monitor competitors, detect and categorize signals, send alerts and weekly briefings
- ZH: 竞争情报智能体：监测竞争对手、识别与归类信号、发送警报和周报
- EN: Battlecard Agent — update battlecards on approved signals, notify sales immediately
- ZH: 对手作战卡智能体：基于确认信号更新 Battlecard 并第一时间通知销售
- EN: Operator Agent — book meetings, prep briefings, convert decisions into tracked tasks, monitor priorities
- ZH: 运营智能体：安排会议、准备简报、将决策转为可追踪任务并监控优先级
- EN: Org Health Agent — scan for revenue risks, cost leaks, and failing initiatives
- ZH: 组织健康智能体：扫描营收风险、成本漏斗与执行受阻的项目
- EN: Strategy Consultant Agent — identify growth opportunities, generate action plans
- ZH: 战略咨询智能体：识别增长机会并输出行动方案

## 在 monday 里运行 Claude 的四种方式 / Four ways to run Claude in monday

> EN: Customers use Claude inside the monday platform through four capabilities:
>
> ZH: 客户可以通过四种能力在 monday 平台中使用 Claude：

> EN: With monday Agents, teams can build custom agents using prompts, and choose Claude as its model. The platform gives the agent a name, a face, and a place on the board where anyone can assign it work.
>
> ZH: 使用 monday Agents 时，团队可通过 Prompt 构建专属智能体，并选用 Claude 作为底层模型；平台为其分配名称、头像与看板位置，任何人都可在其上派发任务。

> EN: Bring Your Own Agent (BYOA) makes it possible for Claude Managed Agents to join the platform. Once on the monday platform, an agent one person has built can become a teammate the whole team can mention and assign work to.
>
> ZH: 带入自建智能体（BYOA）让 Claude Managed Agents 能加入平台。这样，某位成员在外部构建的智能体可成为全团队可见、可提及、可派工的同伴。

> EN: Pre-built Agents, available in the monday Agents Store, turn Claude plugins into specialized teammates: a legal team can run a legal plugin as an agent inside its own workflows, and finance teams can do the same with theirs.
>
> ZH: monday Agents Store 提供可直接使用的预置智能体，把 Claude 插件转化为特定场景同伴；法务团队可在自身流程中用法务插件形成智能体，财务团队也能用自己的插件。

> EN: The Claude Coding integration enables teams to connect Claude in the monday dashboard, then plan and assign agents tasks. Claude Managed Agents executes in the customer's own environment, and results and updates land back on the ticket before the task hands off to the next agent or to a human for review. The work runs from business need to working code and back to the business user.
>
> ZH: Claude Coding 集成让团队可在 monday 控制台直接连接 Claude、制定并分配任务。Claude Managed Agents 在客户自有环境执行后，会将结果回写工单，再交给下一位智能体或交给人工复核，完成从业务需求到可运行代码再回到业务用户的闭环。

## 从需求到登录页，不离开看板 / From brief to landing page without leaving the board

> EN: One end-to-end example: a marketing team runs a campaign production line inside a single board item. The marketer and content lead shape the brief on the item, aligning on goal, audience, key message, and channels. A Strategist Agent built with monday Agents turns that raw input into a structured brief covering the campaign objective, messaging pillars, channel breakdown, and success metrics.
>
> ZH: 一个端到端案例：市场团队在单一看板项中完成整个活动生产线。市场人员和内容负责人在该条目里协作定义目标、受众、核心信息和渠道。由 monday Agents 构建的 Strategist Agent 会将这些输入结构化为完整 brief，包含活动目标、信息主轴、渠道拆解与成功指标。

> EN: From there, a Landing Page Builder takes over. Running on Claude Managed Agents in the company's own environment, it pulls the approved brief and generates a new variant of an existing landing page, with copy, structure, and messaging adapted to the campaign. The output lands back on the monday item automatically. Before the page reaches approval, a Brand Reviewer, a Claude Managed Agent, checks it against brand guidelines and legal standards and flags anything that needs human attention. The marketing manager then makes one decision: publish or refine.
>
> ZH: 随后由 Landing Page Builder 接手。该能力运行在客户自有环境中的 Claude Managed Agents 上，从已批准的 brief 中生成着陆页新版本（文案、结构、表达都针对本活动优化），输出会自动回写回看板项。页面进入审批前，Brand Reviewer 智能体（同样基于 Claude Managed Agents）会对照品牌规范和法务标准进行检查并标记待人类处理项，最后由市场经理决定“发布”或“继续优化”。

## 家族业务迈向 AI 前沿 / A family business at the AI frontier

> EN: Cooke, a family seafood business founded in 1985 in Blacks Harbour, New Brunswick, has grown from a single farm site with 5,000 salmon into the world's largest family-owned seafood company, operating in 16 countries. Today, Cooke runs project delivery, resource management, and contract management on Claude and monday together. Product managers use Claude to turn approved charters and requirements into initial project plans, generate status reports, and surface risks and issues that feed straight into their monday RAID logs, across roughly 200 active and proposed projects. Claude automates the reporting and data prep that keep lifecycle workflows accurate across 130 contracts—upkeep work that used to be tedious and manual.
>
> ZH: 创立于 1985 年加拿大 New Brunswick 的 Blacks Harbour 的家族企业 Cooke，从单一养殖场（5000 条鲑鱼）发展为在 16 个国家运营的全球最大家族海鲜公司。如今，Cooke 将项目交付、资源管理、合同管理全部放在 Claude 与 monday 的联合体系中。产品经理会用 Claude 将已批准的授权与需求转为初始项目计划、生成状态报告，并识别风险与问题，直接同步到 monday 的 RAID 日志；当前约有 200 个在建及拟建项目。Claude 还在 130 份合同上自动化生成报告与数据准备工作，这些任务过去非常耗时且多为人工操作。

> EN: “Together, monday and Claude help us read team capacity and make smarter allocation calls,” says Patti Stevens, director of strategy at Cooke. “Monday used to be a platform we had to update. Now we operate from it.”
>
> ZH: Cooke 的战略总监 Patti Stevens 表示：“monday 与 Claude 帮我们更准确地读取团队产能并做出更聪明的资源分配决策。以前 Monday 是我们需要不断更新的工具，现在它变成我们工作的主平台。”

## monday.com 的经验教训 / What monday.com learned

> EN: For companies planning a similar rebuild, monday’s team shares five lessons they learned as they transformed their platform into an AI-first product:
>
> ZH: 对于也想走同样重构路径的公司，monday 团队总结了五条经验：

- EN: The mental model is harder to change than the technology. People naturally want to protect quality and keep improving what already works. Moving teams from "how do we responsibly improve the current product?" to "how do we responsibly rebuild it for a different future?" took longer than the technical work.
- ZH: 思维模型比技术实现更难改变。人们本能上希望在现有成果上迭代，不愿放弃既有成功模型。把团队从“如何负责地优化当前产品”转向“如何负责任地为全新未来重建产品”，比技术改造本身更耗时。
- EN: Small teams move faster when everything is changing at the same time. Direction, UX, technology, pricing, the trust model, and the company's own definition of good were all in motion at the same time. Layers of stakeholders would lose that much detail, but small teams with clear ownership and fast decision rights stayed close to it.
- ZH: 当方向、体验、技术、定价、信任模型和“好产品”的定义都同时在变化时，小团队反而能更快推进。大而复杂的组织容易在细节中迷失，而明确负责、决策链路短的小团队更容易始终对齐。
- EN: Adoption depends on trust as much as it does on capability. Product-market fit depends on user confidence and preparedness to let agents into how work actually gets done. Governance, permissions, transparency, and reliability determine whether agents move beyond pilot programs and into production.
- ZH: 采用率取决于信任程度同样多地来源于“愿意”而非“能做”。只有当用户信心建立、并准备好让智能体进入真实工作环节时，产品市场匹配才成立。治理、权限、透明度和稳定性决定了智能体能否从试点走向生产。
- EN: Capability needs infrastructure to match. Agents perform at a different level when they're grounded in live project data, team history, and structured workflows, and at enterprise scale the backend has to hold. Alongside the agent layer, monday invested in monday DB so the data infrastructure could support the volume, speed, and complexity of agents operating across an organization.
- ZH: 能力必须有基础设施支撑。只有将智能体与实时项目数据、团队历史和结构化工作流绑定，AI 才能发挥更高水平。按企业规模落地时，底层后端必须足够强。monday 因此同步投入 monday DB，以支撑跨组织规模下智能体交互的体量、速度和复杂性。
- EN: Build on what already works. monday has always described itself as the place where people team up to drive business outcomes, and the agent-first rebuild extends that promise to a new kind of team member. People still come to monday to achieve their goals, the difference is that some of the team members working alongside them are now agents.
- ZH: 继续构建已有有效的东西。monday 一直以来定位自己是让人们协作达成业务结果的平台，而这次以智能体为先的重建只是把“新型队友”也纳入了这个协作系统。人们依旧来用 monday 实现目标，只是现在一部分协作对象是智能体。
