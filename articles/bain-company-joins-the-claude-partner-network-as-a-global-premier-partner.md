> **EN:** Bain & Company and Anthropic today announced a global partnership to help enterprises move from AI experiments to deployments that produce measurable business results. Bain joins the Claude Partner Network as a Global Premier partner, and the two firms will work together on client engagements spanning AI strategy, technology modernization, and AI-enabled operations.

Bain & Company 与 Anthropic 今日宣布全球合作，目标是帮助企业把 AI 实验阶段推进到能够产出可量化业务成效的落地阶段。Bain 成为 Claude Partner Network 的 Global Premier Partner，双方将联动承接涵盖 AI 战略、技术现代化和 AI 运营化的客户项目。

> **EN:** The partnership builds on Bain's own deployment of Claude across the firm, which now serves as a reference point for how Bain advises clients on enterprise rollouts.

该合作也建立在 Bain 内部大规模采用 Claude 的实践上，Bain 已将其作为指导客户企业部署经验的样板。

## 贝恩如何使用 Claude / How Bain uses Claude

> **EN:** Bain rolled out Claude to all 19,000 of its employees as part of the firm's suite of AI platforms and tools, for use in both client work and internal operations. Teams work with Claude.ai, Claude Cowork, Claude Code, Claude for Excel, and Claude for Microsoft 365 to build and test complex models, conduct research, pressure-test inputs and assumptions, and explore multiple business scenarios.

Bain 将 Claude 在全公司范围内推广到 19,000 名员工，既用于客户交付，也用于内部运营。团队在不同场景下使用 Claude.ai、Claude Cowork、Claude Code、Claude for Excel 和 Claude for Microsoft 365，承担建模、研究、假设压力测试和多方案业务探索。

> **EN:** Adoption came quickly. In the pilot phase alone, more than 7,000 Bain employees were actively working with Claude within a few weeks of getting access, and more than two-thirds of pilot participants adopted Claude for Excel.

落地速度非常快：仅试点期内，7000 多名 Bain 员工在获得权限后的数周内就开始活跃使用 Claude；其中超过三分之二的参与者在工作流中采用了 Claude for Excel。

> **EN:** The rollout paired access with enablement and governance. Employees received onboarding materials, trainings, and webinars alongside expert support, and the firm tracked user experience and feedback through ongoing surveys. That structure, rather than access alone, is what Bain points to when advising clients on their own deployments.

这次推进不仅是开通权限，更配套了培训与治理机制。员工可获得入职材料、训练课程和专题网络研讨会，并在专家支持下持续迭代；同时公司通过持续调研追踪体验与反馈。Bain 建议客户也要重视“训练和治理”，而不只看授权放开本身。

## 对客户意味着什么 / What the partnership means for clients

> **EN:** Clients get Claude models combined with Bain's cross-industry experience in business transformation and AI deployment. Bain's digital teams include more than 1,500 AI, data, analytics, architecture, and engineering experts who work alongside the firm's industry and capability practices.

对客户而言，这意味着可以把 Claude 的模型能力与 Bain 跨行业的业务转型与 AI 落地经验结合起来。Bain 的数字团队拥有 1500 多名 AI、数据、分析、架构和工程专家，与行业与能力实践团队协同作业。

> **EN:** The results so far suggest what that combination can do. Across multiple client engagements involving complex legacy codebases that lack existing architectural context, Bain has helped clients achieve productivity gains of 30% to 50%.

目前案例显示该组合具有明显价值：在多个涉及复杂遗留代码、缺少现成架构文档的项目中，Bain 帮助客户实现了 30% 到 50% 的生产率提升。

> **EN:** "Our partnership with Anthropic brings together its frontier AI technology with Bain's deep strategic and industry expertise to help clients turn the potential of AI into meaningful business results," Philippe d'Arabian, Executive Vice President and Global Head of Partnerships at Bain & Company, says.

Bain & Company 全球合作与战略副总裁 Philippe d'Arabian 表示：这次与 Anthropic 的合作把 Anthropic 的前沿 AI 技术与 Bain 深厚的行业与战略咨询经验结合起来，目标是帮助客户把 AI 潜能转化成真实可衡量的商业结果。

## 下一步 / What's next

> **EN:** Bain and Anthropic are already working together on client engagements across multiple industries. As the partnership develops, the firms expect to expand their collaboration wherever the highest-value opportunities exist.

双方已经在多个行业的客户项目上开始协同。合作深入后，预计会优先在价值密度最高的领域持续扩展。

> **EN:** "More than 7,000 Bain employees were actively using Claude within weeks of the firm-wide deployment. The speed and breadth of that adoption signal what's possible when the right technology is paired with the expertise to deploy it effectively, and that's exactly what this partnership delivers for Bain's employees and clients," says Steve Corfield, global head of business development and partnerships at Anthropic.

Anthropic 全球业务发展与合作负责人 Steve Corfield 表示：Bain 在全公司级部署后数周内已有 7000 多人活跃使用 Claude。这一速度与规模说明，先进技术若能被正确部署和治理，就能形成真正的业务价值；这正是此次合作要为 Bain 员工与客户共同带来的结果。
