## 合作从试验走向落地 / From experiments to deployments

<!-- bilingual:section -->

<!-- lang:zh -->

Bain & Company 与 Anthropic 今日宣布建立全球合作，帮助企业从 AI 实验迈向能够产生可量化业务成果的部署。Bain 加入 Claude Partner Network，成为 Global Premier partner；双方将共同参与涵盖 AI 战略、技术现代化和 AI 赋能运营的客户项目。

这项合作建立在 Bain 在公司内部部署 Claude 的基础之上。这一实践如今也成为 Bain 为客户提供企业级推广建议时的参考样本。

<!-- lang:en -->

Bain & Company and Anthropic today announced a global partnership to help enterprises move from AI experiments to deployments that produce measurable business results. Bain joins the Claude Partner Network as a Global Premier partner, and the two firms will work together on client engagements spanning AI strategy, technology modernization, and AI-enabled operations.

The partnership builds on Bain's own deployment of Claude across the firm, which now serves as a reference point for how Bain advises clients on enterprise rollouts.

<!-- /bilingual:section -->

## 贝恩如何使用 Claude / How Bain uses Claude

<!-- bilingual:section -->

<!-- lang:zh -->

Bain 将 Claude 作为公司 AI 平台与工具套件的一部分，推广给全公司 19,000 名员工，既用于客户工作，也用于内部运营。团队使用 Claude.ai、Claude Cowork、Claude Code、Claude for Excel 和 Claude for Microsoft 365，构建和测试复杂模型、开展研究、对输入与假设进行压力测试，并探索多种业务情景。

Claude 的采用进展迅速。仅在试点阶段，超过 7,000 名 Bain 员工便在获得使用权限后的数周内积极使用 Claude；超过三分之二的试点参与者采用了 Claude for Excel。

这次推广将使用权限与赋能措施及治理机制结合起来。员工在获得专家支持的同时，也接受入职材料、培训和网络研讨会；公司还通过持续开展的调查追踪用户体验与反馈。Bain 在为客户提供部署建议时强调，真正发挥作用的是这套结构，而不只是开放访问权限。

<!-- lang:en -->

Bain rolled out Claude to all 19,000 of its employees as part of the firm's suite of AI platforms and tools, for use in both client work and internal operations. Teams work with Claude.ai, Claude Cowork, Claude Code, Claude for Excel, and Claude for Microsoft 365 to build and test complex models, conduct research, pressure-test inputs and assumptions, and explore multiple business scenarios.

Adoption came quickly. In the pilot phase alone, more than 7,000 Bain employees were actively working with Claude within a few weeks of getting access, and more than two-thirds of pilot participants adopted Claude for Excel.

The rollout paired access with enablement and governance. Employees received onboarding materials, trainings, and webinars alongside expert support, and the firm tracked user experience and feedback through ongoing surveys. That structure, rather than access alone, is what Bain points to when advising clients on their own deployments.

## 对客户意味着什么 / What the partnership means for clients

<!-- bilingual:section -->

<!-- lang:zh -->

客户可以将 Claude 模型与 Bain 在业务转型和 AI 部署方面的跨行业经验结合起来。Bain 的数字团队包括 1,500 多名 AI、数据、分析、架构和工程专家，他们与公司的行业团队及能力实践团队协同工作。

目前取得的成果展现了这种组合的潜力。在多个涉及复杂遗留代码库、且缺乏现有架构背景的客户项目中，Bain 已帮助客户实现 30% 至 50% 的生产率提升。

> “我们与 Anthropic 的合作，将其前沿 AI 技术与 Bain 深厚的战略和行业专长结合起来，帮助客户把 AI 的潜力转化为有意义的业务成果。”Bain & Company 执行副总裁兼全球合作负责人 Philippe d'Arabian 表示。

<!-- lang:en -->

Clients get Claude models combined with Bain's cross-industry experience in business transformation and AI deployment. Bain's digital teams include more than 1,500 AI, data, analytics, architecture, and engineering experts who work alongside the firm's industry and capability practices.

The results so far suggest what that combination can do. Across multiple client engagements involving complex legacy codebases that lack existing architectural context, Bain has helped clients achieve productivity gains of 30% to 50%.

> "Our partnership with Anthropic brings together its frontier AI technology with Bain's deep strategic and industry expertise to help clients turn the potential of AI into meaningful business results," Philippe d'Arabian, Executive Vice President and Global Head of Partnerships at Bain & Company, says.
