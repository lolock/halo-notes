## 为什么现在必须学习 AI？ / Why is it important to learn about AI now?

> EN: As model intelligence continues to evolve, the need for everyone to understand how to use AI safely and effectively becomes more urgent. Not only does AI education help people accomplish more, it helps them make informed decisions about how they want AI to show up in their communities and workplaces.
>
> ZH: 随着模型智能持续演进，所有人都要懂得如何安全且有效地使用 AI 的需求日益迫切。AI 教育不仅能帮助人们更高效地完成工作，也能帮助他们在社区和职场中做出关于 AI 应该如何介入的更明智决策。

> EN: Learning to use AI will help individuals and organizations take advantage of the potential benefits of AI while mitigating some of the risk. This includes things like reducing manual work, unlocking new ways of working, and upskilling in new areas.
>
> ZH: 学会使用 AI，个人和组织可以在降低部分风险的同时，充分利用 AI 带来的潜力，包括减少手工工作、开辟新的工作方式，并在新领域快速提升能力。

## Claude Academy 的设计与组织化课程，与 Anthropic 内部教育保持一致 / Claude Academy instruction mirrors Anthropic’s approach to educating its own employees

> EN: At Anthropic, we believe the journey to AI fluency begins on an employee’s first day. During onboarding, we teach all employees the 4D AI Fluency Framework, best practices on managing what agents know, and how fast the AI exponential moves. Learners practice making intentional decisions about what tasks should be done by AI and what tasks they should do themselves. They also learn the mistakes that AI tends to make so they can review AI-generated work more effectively. After onboarding, we offer a variety of “ever-boarding” (always-on onboarding) programs that deeply explore AI’s capabilities and limitations as well as evidence-based practices for working on human-agent teams. In a field moving this fast, continuous learning is an expectation for every role.
>
> ZH: 在 Anthropic，我们认为 AI 能力的养成从入职第一天就要开始。入职阶段会为每位员工讲解 4D AI Fluency Framework（AI 素养框架）、如何管理智能体已知内容的最佳实践，以及 AI 发展速度为什么会如此快。学习者会练习主动判断哪些任务适合 AI、哪些任务必须由自己完成，并学习 AI 常见容易出现的错误，以便更有效地审阅 AI 输出。入职后，我们还会提供一系列“持续入职（ever-boarding）”课程，持续深入探索 AI 的能力与边界，以及基于证据的人机协同工作方法。这个领域变化极快，持续学习已成为每个岗位的基本要求。

> EN: In addition to live and online instruction, we also provide all Anthropic employees with Claude-powered tools (like Claude Tag) to ensure they get instant answers to virtually any question related to the company, their job, or their specific onboarding plan. We also have Claude-moderated Slack channels for IT, legal, and benefits so people can focus on what matters most.
>
> ZH: 除了线上和线下课程，我们还给所有 Anthropic 员工提供 Claude 赋能的工具（如 Claude Tag），让他们能在几乎任何与公司、岗位或入职计划相关的问题上即时获得答案。我们还设置了 IT、法务和福利相关的 Claude 协同 Slack 频道，让大家把精力集中在最重要的事项上。

> EN: When employees become AI fluent, we see them forming teams flexibly around real problems, taking on projects with expanded scope, and learning from company experts at scale. For Anthropic, AI fluency is the foundation required to have impact

## 拆解我们的教学方式 / Breaking down our approach

> EN: In Claude Academy, all educational materials are designed to be broadly accessible, facilitating access to the most current information on how to work with AI and how AI works. Of course we offer learning about how to use Claude, but we also offer product and model agnostic learning that democratizes key information about this new technology that will have a wide-ranging impact on the world. In addition, we adhere to the following learning design principles.
>
> ZH: 在 Claude Academy 中，所有课程材料都以更广泛的可及性为目标，帮助学习者理解“如何与 AI 协同工作”以及“AI 的运作机制”。当然，我们提供了大量 Claude 使用教程，但也提供与具体产品无关、面向模型通用的学习内容，让更多人接触到这项将重塑世界的关键技术。与此同时，我们始终坚持以下学习设计原则。

### AI 教育应提升人类能动性 / AI education should increase human agency

> EN: Learning to use AI well should open up new doors and opportunities for students. Our learning materials are designed to help learners solve the problems they care about most. Instead of simply offering learning that shows you how to use our products and features, our education is centered on the problems you face at work and in life. Of note, our educational materials also encourage mindfulness about continuing to practice skills that matter to you in order to prevent skill atrophy. For example, this collection of legal use cases teaches you how to use Claude while also offering you the chance to reflect on what tasks should stay with you.
>
> ZH: 学会善用 AI 应该为学习者打开新的机会与路径。我们的课程并不只是教你如何操作功能，而是围绕你在工作与生活中最关心的问题来设计。学习材料还会提醒大家持续练习重要技能，防止能力退化。例如，关于法律场景的一组案例，不仅教你如何使用 Claude，也引导你反思哪些任务仍应由自己来做。

### 认知心态很关键 / Mindsets matter

> EN: Given how fast AI is changing, mastering features alone won’t lead to lasting AI fluency. Even specific behaviors like “describe your audience” that were once useful have become less important when using the newest models. For example, where you used to have to tell Claude that your task is intended for “colleagues working in legal” now Claude will simply ask you for that information if it’s needed for a high quality output. Our education team has shifted away from emphasizing specific behaviors for AI fluency towards cultivating broader, more durable mindsets for using AI. Things like “today’s AI is the worst AI you’ll ever use” and “verify in proportion to the stakes” help people exercise good judgement with AI even as products and features change.
>
> ZH: 鉴于 AI 变化极快，单纯掌握某些功能并不能带来长期的 AI 流畅度。像“先描述受众”这样的具体动作，以前曾很常用，但在新模型中已不再是核心：例如你过去需要说明任务面向“法律同事”，现在 Claude 在高质量输出需要时会自己追问。我们的教学团队因此不再强调一组固定操作动作，而是培养更持久、可迁移的使用心智，例如“今天的 AI 是你未来将会使用过的最差版本”，以及“按风险高低对应地核验”。这些观念能帮助人们在工具和功能不断变化时仍保持判断力。

### 安全高效的 AI 使用远不止与 AI 的对话时刻 / Safe and effective AI use extends far beyond the interactions someone has with AI

> EN: Most learning about AI focuses almost exclusively on a user’s conversation with an agent. While that’s critical, we also believe that the moments surrounding AI use are just as important. For example, we encourage learners to ask themselves what tasks should be delegated to AI in the first place and which tasks should stay with them. For example, you may want to draft sensitive parts of a memo but leave putting together summary slides to AI. Or you may want Claude to help with some exploratory data analysis but do final checks yourself. We also educate learners on how to ethically disclose AI use to colleagues, customers, and other stakeholders. This includes explicitly stating how AI was used in the production of documents, analyses, or media before you share it with others. This broader perspective ensures that learners build an intentional relationship with AI from the start.
>
> ZH: 大多数 AI 学习都几乎只关注用户与智能体的对话过程，但我们认为对话前后的环节同样重要。我们鼓励学习者先判断哪些任务应交给 AI、哪些应由自己完成。例如你可以自己起草备忘录中的敏感段落，而把总结幻灯片交给 AI；或者让 Claude 做一些探索性数据分析，但最终核对留给自己。我们也教导大家如何向同事、客户和相关方合规透明地披露 AI 的参与方式，比如在共享文档、分析报告或媒体内容前，明确说明其中 AI 的作用。这样的更完整视角有助于从一开始就建立“有意识地使用 AI”的关系。

### 学习需要持续投入 / Learning takes effort

> EN: Our use cases, tutorials, and courses encourage you to practice with Claude as you go. Our learning exercises encourage reflection and experimentation so learners can discover what works best for them when using AI. Of note, we believe that today’s Claude Academy experience is the most rigid it’ll ever be. With Claude, we’ll be able to deliver very personalized learning activities and exercises at a scale that hasn’t been possible before.
>
> ZH: Claude Academy 的案例、教程与课程都要求学习者在使用中不断实践。课程任务鼓励反思和试验，让学习者逐步找到最适合自己的 AI 使用方式。值得强调的是，我们认为当前版本的 Claude Academy 是此类体验中最稳定、可依赖的阶段；随着 Claude 的进步，日后能以更大规模提供更加个性化的学习活动与练习。
