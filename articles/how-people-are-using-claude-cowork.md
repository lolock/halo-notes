---
2025 年 Claude Code 发布时，我们惊讶地发现许多非技术用户开始尝试使用它。从未打开过终端的人竟然用它来创建整理文件夹、去重文件、编写电子表格公式的 agent。

> EN: When we released Claude Code in 2025, we were surprised at how many non-technical users started playing around with it. People who had never opened the terminal before were using it to create agents that organized folders, deduplicated files, and wrote spreadsheet formulas.

但对其他人来说，终端本身就是障碍。不是每个人都在终端里工作，大部分人不在。

> EN: But for others, the terminal itself was the barrier. Not everyone works in a terminal, and most people don't.

于是 2026 年 2 月我们发布了 Claude Cowork——一个面向所有人的 agent。这次没有终端。它是一个不论是设计师、产品经理还是运营人员都会感觉像办公软件（他们实际使用的办公软件）的 AI agent。

> EN: So in February 2026, we shipped Claude Cowork — an agent for everyone. No terminal this time. An AI agent that feels like the office software designers, program managers, and operations people actually use, because it lives in the desktop app they already have.

六个月过去，Cowork 正在做 Claude Code 做不到的事：将 agent 能力带给营销、财务、运营、HR、销售和支持团队。个体贡献者和高级领导者都在使用它。

> EN: Six months in, Cowork is doing something Claude Code couldn't: bringing agentic behavior to marketing, finance, operations, HR, sales, and support teams. Individual contributors and senior leaders alike are using it.

如今我们回顾用户是如何使用 Cowork 的——他们托管了哪些工作，以及为什么这种模式指向了一个新的工作未来。

> EN: Today we're looking back at how people have used Cowork — what kind of work they've delegated, and why this pattern points to a new future of work.

## 90% 不是写代码 / Over 90% isn't coding

从 cowriting 一篇帖子到深入研究一整套客户发现通话记录，人们使用 Cowork 的方式令人意外。

> EN: From cowriting a post to doing deep research across a set of customer discovery call transcripts, people have used Cowork in surprising ways.

我们对其中的所有任务进行了分类，绝大多数不是写代码。事实上，超过 90% 是知识工作——文档、分析、创作和沟通。

> EN: We categorized all the tasks people delegate to Cowork, and the vast majority aren't coding. In fact, more than 90% is knowledge work — documents, analysis, content creation, and communication.

### 人们在委托的业务职能 / The business functions people are delegating

Cowork 中被委托任务最多的职能包括：

- 客户成功案例研究：从通话记录中构建案例研究
- 准备好下周的管道审查——“把所有方框连起来”
- 合同审查，包括将大数拆解为消耗性构建模块
- 自动研究每日和每周的行业简报

> EN: The top functions where tasks get delegated in Cowork include building case studies from call transcripts, getting ready for next week's pipeline review by "connecting all the boxes," contract review that breaks big numbers into consumable building blocks, and automated research for daily and weekly industry briefs.

大多数任务（称为"委托"）是由 Claude 与用户所连接的数十种工具协作完成的：日历、Google Workspace、Slack、Salesforce 等等。这些不是聊天，而是真正的 agent 行为：Claude 自己使用工具、读写文件、或者像真人同事一样寻找事实。

> EN: Most tasks—which they call delegations—are completed by Claude working across dozens of tools the user has connected: calendar, Google Workspace, Slack, Salesforce, and more. These aren't chats. They're true agent behavior: Claude using tools on its own, reading and writing files, looking up facts the way a human colleague would.

## 是什么让一个好的委托任务 / What makes a good delegation

那么什么样的工作适合委托给 agent？我们回顾了人们委托的成千上万条任务，成功模式呈现出一个非常清晰的形态：委托任务一般是结果导向的（"给我 X"），但设定在灵活的约束条件下（"根据当前管道，使用最好用的数据"）。

> EN: So what kind of work gets delegated? We looked back at tens of thousands of tasks people handed to Cowork, and what's landed has a very clear shape. A delegation is generally output-oriented ("get me X") but set against flexible constraints ("use whatever is best based on current pipeline data").

委托任务有自然边界——而不是一次性的大量读取——Claude 可以按照直接的里程碑规划路径：读取这些文件，检查此来源，给出初步结论让你审查，然后进行迭代。

> EN: Delegations have natural boundaries instead of one massive read — concrete milestones Claude can plan a path to: read these files, check this source, come back with a preliminary finding for you to review, then do the final output.

人们会注意将正确的工作交给 Claude：审慎、上下文丰富的活动，以及时间丰富的机械工作。人类做前者，Claude 做后者。

> EN: People are careful to give Claude the right work: judgment, context-rich activities vs. time-intensive mechanical work. Humans do the former, Claude does the latter.

## 委托正在加速 / Delegation is accelerating

人们适应了委托、写作和审阅的节奏后，开始自行找到节奏：

- 开始的一天或一周先委托 Claude 构建一个简报包：会议笔记、电子邮件草稿以及他们需要的其他内容。
- 将更多从想法到初稿的阶段外包出去。
- 大多数人从简单的小任务开始，稳步建立起协同工作的直觉，然后转向更大的任务。

> EN: As people settle into the rhythm of delegate-write-review-delegate, they find their own cadence:
> Starting their day or week by delegating Claude to build a briefing pack: meeting notes, email drafts, and whatever else they need.
> Outsourcing more of the ideation-to-draft phase.
> Starting with small, simple tasks, steadily building that muscle for collaborative work, then turning to bigger tasks.

按人均分配看，6 个月间委托量增加了 72%。部分原因是信任的建立——人们看到了结果并信任更大型任务。部分原因是 Cowork 本身变得更好了：更快的上下文检索、对信息和协作更好的处理，以及更好的任务后总结。

> EN: Per-user delegation volume increased 72X over six months. Partly it's trust that's been built. People see results and trust bigger tasks. Partly it's that Cowork itself got better: faster context retrieval, better handling of information and collaboration, and better post-task summaries.

## 精英用户做对的事 / What heavy users get right

在重度用户中，有一个共同的行为特征：他们委托目标时直接给出对结果的精确定义。像"完成（好的）外部推送编辑稿"这样，已经表明他们对什么算"完成"有强烈的直觉。

> EN: Across heavy users, there's a shared behavioral trait: they describe tasks with a precise definition of success — "done editing this external comms piece (the good kind)" — showing they already have a strong intuition for what "finished" should look like.

这群人倾向于将纯机械编辑或纯信息挖掘的东西外包给 Claude，同时保留需要人类品味的判断密集型部分。

> EN: This group tends to outsource purely mechanical edits or purely information-mining stuff to Claude while keeping the judgment-intensive parts that require human taste.

他们还更快地摸清了"检查 Claude 的工作 - 纠正 - 转向 - 完成"的循环回路。他们不只是用一遍就停下来；他们会进行迭代，形成每 1-2 次会审核一次并给予即时自己方向回馈的节奏。

> EN: They also figured out the loop of checking Claude's work, correcting, steering, and finishing faster. They don't just use it once and stop; they iterate, establishing a cadence of reviewing and giving immediate directional feedback with every one or two turn.

速度快的人从不超过两个回合没有他们就回复 Claude。速度对此回路至关重要：如果一个转折在期望电话中停留太久，上下文就会过期，节奏就会断掉。保持回路快速运转就是秘诀。

> EN: Power users almost never let more than two turns go by without a reply. Speed matters for this loop: if a turn sits for too long, context expires and rhythm breaks. Keeping the loop moving is the secret.

重度用户常常给 Claude 设立"约束沙箱"——"你不要动幻灯片的方框文案，只处理语言本身"——因为他们已经知道答案不在那些框里。

> EN: Heavy users often give Claude a "constraint sandbox" — "you don't control the box copy in the deck, please just touch the language" — when they already know that the answer isn't in those boxes.

而且，在打开 Cowork 两小时之内，最活跃的用户完成的工作量超出了他们自己预期的上限。

> EN: And, in two hours after they open it, most active people get more done than they ever expected.

## 所以工作变成什么样了？ / So what does work become?

如果你把不需要你的部分给自己的代理，留给自己需要你自己的部分，工作会变成这样：早上 8 点，你拿起手机，Claude 已经制作了一份简报文档，总结了昨天的 Slack 中所有相关项目的更新、附了邮件草稿，并整理了一份当天下午的客户会议计划。你用十分钟评论通话记录稿，Claude 接手推荐回应，然后你去吃早餐。

> EN: If you give the agent the parts of your work that don't need you, and keep the parts that do, work starts to look like this: at 8 a.m., you grab your phone, and Claude has already built a briefing doc with the relevant Slack project updates from yesterday, email drafts attached, and a plan for your client meeting this afternoon. You spend ten minutes commenting on the call transcript, Claude takes over the recommendations, and you go grab breakfast.

这个观念本身并非全新。在此之前，大部分知识工作者只是缺少合适的工具来将其实现。Cowork 正在让它成为现实，并且承担了大多数人在角色中被动态模式消耗的大量时间。

> EN: The idea itself isn't new. Until now, most knowledge workers simply lacked the right tools to make it real. Cowork is making it real, and it's taking on a growing share of the hours most people spend in reactive mode at their roles.

Cowork 仍处于早期阶段。规模化带来新的问题——安全、控制、工作存放的位置、以及如何审计被无数无形之手触碰过的工作——这些是我们小心对待、并将在后续稳步发布的领域，无需喧嚣。

> EN: Cowork is still in its early stages. Scale brings new questions — security, control, where the work lives, and how you audit work that dozens of unseen hands have touched — areas we're holding carefully and will ship steadily, without needing to be loud.
